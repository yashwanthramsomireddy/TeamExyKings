Your secrets, your money and your miles.
One private app.

King Log keeps your banking, card, password and WiFi vault, your salary and savings plan, and your bike and car fuel log behind your own lock. Your data stays on your phone, backups are encrypted, and the app is free, ad-free and subscription-free, with no tracking.

Vault

Banking, Cards, Passwords and WiFi, encrypted on your phone.

  • Entries encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore
  • Secrets hidden, copied passwords clear after 30 seconds
  • Password generator, card-expiry warnings, WiFi QR code
  • Favourites, categories, search that never reads secrets
  • Government IDs kept safely in Passwords

Finance

Know what lands in the bank and what you keep, month by month.

  • Simple and Pro salary calculators
  • Plan future months, copy forward 1 to 12 months or more
  • Months tab with changes, yearly summary and analytics
  • Gratuity, PVL per day and PI helpers
  • EMI plan: loans, SIPs and bills by month, pre-close costs, Excel export and import
  • Numbers encrypted on your phone

Fuel Log

Bikes and cars, every fill-up, real mileage.

  • Full-to-full mileage, cost per km, price per litre
  • Monthly spend, mileage and cost trends

  • Insurance and service reminders (optional)

Backups you can trust

One tap. Everything. Encrypted so only you can open it.

  1. Back up nowFinance, EMI plan, Fuel and Vault are saved together in a dated folder, and every file is read back and checked.
  2. Your recovery keyKing Log makes a key like KING-7F3K-9Q2M-XD84-H5RT. Finance, EMI plan and Vault files are locked with it. Not Google, not us.
  3. Safe by defaultA weekly backup on your phone is always on. Add Google Drive or a folder of your choice if you like.
  4. Restore in a minutePick a date, see what is inside, choose Merge or Replace. A safety copy is kept before any Replace.

Honest notes

If you lose both your phone and your recovery key, the encrypted backups cannot be opened by anyone, including us. The Fuel log is not encrypted: it holds vehicle and fill-up details only.
