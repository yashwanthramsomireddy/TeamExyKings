1.Who we are

DebloatKit is a Windows desktop application published by TeamExyKings, an independent developer based in Chennai, India (author: Yashwanth Ram Somireddy). In this policy "we", "us" and "DebloatKit" mean TeamExyKings and the application.

2.What this policy covers

This policy covers the DebloatKit application (v2.0) and this website (the pages index.html, privacy-policy.html and terms-of-service.html). DebloatKit is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd.

3.What we collect

Nothing. The application does not collect, transmit or upload personal information, device identifiers, usage statistics, crash reports or package lists to us or to any server. The application contains no analytics or advertising code, and it makes no network requests of its own. We do not operate any server that the application talks to.

4.Data that stays on your computer

To do its job, DebloatKit creates a few things locally. They are stored only on your PC, under your control, and are never sent to us:

ItemWhat it containsWhere
BackupsJSON snapshots made before each action: the phone's model name, a timestamp, and the list of package names with their enabled / disabled / uninstalled state.A backups folder next to the application (or in %LOCALAPPDATA%\DebloatKit\backups if that folder is read-only). Location can be changed in Settings.
LogA running text log of actions and ADB results during the session (it can mention the device model and package names).In memory only. It is written to disk only if you press Export Log and choose a location.
SettingsThe ADB path and backup folder you choose, and the theme for the current session.In memory for the session.

You can delete backups and exported logs at any time like any other file. Uninstalling DebloatKit does not send anything anywhere; delete the backups folder if you also want those files gone.

5.How the app talks to your phone

DebloatKit communicates with your Android device through ADB (Android Debug Bridge), over the USB cable, using the adb program on your computer. To list and manage apps, it reads information such as the device model, brand, Android / One UI version, API level, battery level and the list of installed package names and their state. This information is read on your computer, shown in the app window, and is not transmitted anywhere by DebloatKit. It does not read your messages, photos, contacts, call history, accounts or other personal content, and it does not copy files from your phone.

The ADB program itself (provided by Google as part of Android Platform Tools, not by us) is separate software that is governed by its own terms.

6.Third-party services

DebloatKit does not integrate any third-party SDKs. The only times it connects you to an external service are when you click a link, which opens your default web browser:

  • GitHub — the project page and releases (github.com/yashwanthramsomireddy/Debloat-Kit). GitHub's privacy statement applies once you visit.
  • PayPal and Razorpay — only if you choose to make an optional donation. Payments are processed entirely by those companies under their own privacy policies. We never see or store your card or bank details; we may receive the payer name, amount and any note you add from the payment provider.

The installer, if you use it, is built with Inno Setup and does not send data anywhere.

7.This website

This site is a set of static pages. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, forms, login or any external fonts, scripts or images. It may be hosted on GitHub Pages (or a similar static host), and that host may automatically process technical information such as your IP address and browser details in server logs when you request a page. We do not receive or control that data; see the host's privacy statement. Links on this site to GitHub, PayPal and Razorpay lead to third-party sites with their own policies.

8.Sharing and selling data

Because we do not collect your data, we do not sell, rent, trade or share it. If you contact us by email, we use your address and message only to reply to you.

9.Retention and your control

We hold no personal data about you from the application. Files created locally by DebloatKit remain until you delete them. If you email us, we keep the correspondence only as long as needed to help you and then delete it on request.

10.Security

Since all processing is local, your data does not travel over our systems. Two things are within your control: download DebloatKit only from the official GitHub releases page, and keep USB debugging switched off on your phone whenever you are not using the app, because it grants a connected computer elevated access to the device. Treat backup and exported log files like any other file on your PC.

11.Children

DebloatKit is a developer-oriented utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

12.Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR or similar laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data from the application, there is generally nothing to access or delete, but if you have emailed us you can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time using the contact below.

13.Changes to this policy

If the application's behaviour changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, if an update check or any online feature is ever added), we will update this page and the "last updated" date before releasing that version. Material changes will also be noted in the project's CHANGELOG.
