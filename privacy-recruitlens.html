Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026
🤖 Android 🔍 Business / Recruiting

Short version: RecruitLens is a recruiting tool: your account's data (team details, candidates, resumes, AI analysis results, payment/transaction records) is stored in our database so the app can function for your organization. Resume text is sent to an AI model only when you trigger a scan or use Resume Builder, routed to whichever model you select. We don't run ads, analytics, or sell any data. There is no Google Sign-In in this app — accounts use email and password.

1. Information We Collect

We do not run ad tracking, analytics, or sell your data. What's collected:

  • Your account email, name, and password (stored securely, never in plain text) — to create and secure your account
  • Your organization/domain and team structure, and the role(s) you're assigned — to enforce access permissions
  • Resumes and candidate information uploaded to your account (file content, extracted text, candidate details, status/tags) — the core data the app is built to manage
  • AI analysis results for scanned resumes, and any content generated by Resume Builder
  • Payment and transaction records (credit purchases, Domain License purchases) — payment details themselves are handled by Razorpay, not stored by us (see Section 3)
  • Content of transactional emails sent to you (interview reminders, welcome emails, low-credit notices)

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All account, candidate, and resume data is stored in our backend database (Supabase), encrypted in transit, and access-scoped per your organization and team — users can only see data their role permits. You can request deletion of your account and its data by contacting us.

3. Third-Party Services

RecruitLens uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Our backend provider for authentication, database, storage and server-side functions. Effectively all app data (accounts, candidates, resume text, AI analysis results, transactions, logs) is hosted here.
  • OpenRouter — Used to run AI resume scanning and Resume Builder's restructuring feature. Resume text is sent only when you trigger a scan or restructure, routed to whichever AI model (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or others) you select for that action.
  • Razorpay — Used to process credit purchases and the Domain License fee. Payment is handled entirely by Razorpay; we never see or store your card or bank details. See Razorpay's privacy policy.
  • Brevo — Used to send transactional emails (interview reminders, welcome emails, low-credit notices) to the email address and name on your account, including the specific content of that email.
  • ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) — A public, keyless currency-rate lookup used only by the Super Admin financial dashboard for USD→INR conversion. No user data is sent.

No analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDK is used anywhere in the app.

4. Permissions Used

RecruitLens requests storage access, used to let you pick a resume file to upload (PDF/.doc/.docx) and to save exported files (candidate Excel exports, Resume Builder PDF/Word output) to a folder you choose, and vibration for standard UI feedback. The app's manifest also declares camera and notification permissions as leftovers from its initial template — neither is actually used anywhere in the app (no camera feature exists, and no push notification is ever sent); we intend to remove these in a future update. RecruitLens does not request access to your contacts, SMS, microphone, or location, and has no Google Sign-In or other OAuth integration.

5. If You're a Candidate, Not an Account Holder

If your resume was uploaded to RecruitLens by a recruiter or employer you applied to, you are not the account holder, and we don't have a direct relationship with you. Your resume and any AI-generated analysis of it are stored and processed as described above, on behalf of, and under the control of, the organization that uploaded it. Requests to access, correct, or delete your data should go to that organization; we assist them in fulfilling such requests on a reasonable basis.

6. Monetization

RecruitLens uses one-time credit purchases (consumed per AI action, no auto-renewing subscription) and a one-time annual Domain License for organization-wide access, both processed via Razorpay as described in Section 3.

7. Children's Privacy

RecruitLens is a business tool for recruiters and employers, not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of RecruitLens after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
