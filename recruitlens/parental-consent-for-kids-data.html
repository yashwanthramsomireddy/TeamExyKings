Children's Data and Parental Notice

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: Recruit Lens is for people aged 18 or older. We do not allow children to have an account, so we do not collect children's data and there is no parental-consent route.

1. Who can use Recruit Lens

Recruit Lens is a tool for screening job applicants and building resumes. You must be 18 or older to create an account. The app asks every new person to confirm this, and to accept our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy, before the account is created. We do not ask for a date of birth.

2. We do not knowingly collect data from children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets that age) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. We do not show ads and we do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

3. If you think a child has an account

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has created an account or that their information appears in the app, write to yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com with the child's email address. We will delete the account and its data within 30 days, and sooner when we can. You can also follow the steps on our Delete your account page.

4. Resumes of people under 18

People who upload a resume are responsible for having the right to do so (see our Terms). If you find a resume of a minor that should not be there, write to the same address and we will remove it.
