King Estate – Privacy Policy

App: King Estate (com.teamexykings.kingestate) · Developer: Yashwanth Ram Somireddy (TeamExyKings) · Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Version 1.4.0

In short: King Estate works entirely on your phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics.

1. About this app

King Estate is a free property-document checklist for Indian property transactions. It also includes a Building Kata calculator that estimates construction materials and compares dealer quotes you type in.

3. Government non-affiliation disclaimer

This app is an independent real estate platform and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any government entity. The information displayed inside the app is for reference purposes only.

4. Information we collect

None. The developer never receives any information from the app. Everything you enter stays on your device:

  • checklists, document statuses and personal notes;
  • reminder dates you set;
  • your chosen state, theme, accent colour and Home layout;
  • files you choose to attach to a document (see section 6);
  • values typed into the Building Kata calculator (area, quality level, dealer names and rates), which are kept on your phone so you can continue later and can be cleared with the reset button.

This data is stored on your phone and is not uploaded, synced or backed up to any cloud by us.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+): used only to show reminders you create yourself. You can decline; the app still works.
  • King Estate does not request internet, contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage or phone permissions. Files you attach are picked with Android's own file picker and saved through Android's standard storage service, which needs no storage permission.

6. Files you create or attach

Attachments: when you add a photo, PDF, Word or text file to a document, King Estate saves a copy in your phone's Downloads folder under Downloads / King Estate / transaction type / document name, so you can find it in any Files app. The copy stays on your phone; nothing is uploaded. Removing the file inside King Estate deletes the copy it made. Clear All Data and Restore from backup remove the links but leave the files in Downloads/King Estate, which you can delete with a file manager.

PDF and backup files: when you export a PDF checklist or save a JSON backup, the file is created on your device. You decide whether to share or store it, and with whom (for example by email or WhatsApp). Once you share a file, the receiving app's privacy policy applies. Keep backups safe: they contain your notes.

7. Links to other websites

Some screens show addresses of government portals and links to our website. Opening one launches your browser. Those sites are not controlled by us and have their own policies. The app itself makes no network connection.

8. Analytics, ads and tracking

None. There are no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, session-recording or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Fonts are your phone's built-in system fonts.

9. Children

King Estate is intended for adults dealing with property matters and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

10. Your control and data deletion

  • Delete everything: Settings > Data privacy > Clear All Data, or uninstall the app. Delete any attached files from Downloads/King Estate with a file manager.
  • If you have ever emailed us, write to the address below and we will delete that correspondence within 30 days. The app does not send us any email.

Because the developer holds no data from the app, there is nothing to access, correct or export from our side. Use Settings > Create backup to take your own copy.

11. Security

App data stays in Android's app-private storage and is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. Attached files in Downloads are normal files that other apps with storage access can see, so avoid attaching anything you would not want others to find on a shared phone. We also recommend using a screen lock.

12. Not legal or construction advice

Checklists and calculator outputs are general guides only. Requirements vary by state and property, and calculator figures are indicative thumb rules. Consult a qualified lawyer, registrar or structural engineer before acting.

13. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example if a feature ever uses the internet), we will update this page and the date above, and describe the change in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
