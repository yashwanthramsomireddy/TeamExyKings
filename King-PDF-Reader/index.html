What it does

Smooth continuous reading

Scroll straight through a document. Pages render as you reach them and stay cached, so going back is instant.

Zoom that stays sharp

Pinch or double-tap to zoom up to 500%. Pages are re-rendered at each zoom step instead of being stretched, so text never turns blocky.

Night mode

Invert the page for dark-room reading. On an AMOLED screen that is kinder on the eyes and lighter on the battery.

Bookmarks

Save any page, give it a name, and jump back to it from the bookmarks list.

Recent files

Every document you open is remembered along with the page you reached. Pin the ones you keep coming back to.

Fit width or fit page

Fill the screen width for text, or fit the whole page for slides and scanned forms.

Privacy, in one line

  • No internet permission. The app does not request it, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No accounts. Nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics. None are bundled.
  • Your files stay yours. PDFs are read where they already are and never copied, uploaded or modified.

Good to know

  • Encrypted, password-protected PDFs cannot be opened — the app uses Android's own PDF engine, which does not support them.
  • This app reads PDFs. Editing, filling forms, signing and OCR live in King Craft, a separate app in the same family.
  • Settings, recent files and bookmarks can be backed up to a JSON file and restored on another device.
