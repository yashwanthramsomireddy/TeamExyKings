King Six — Delete your account and data

App by TeamExyKings (Yashwanth Ram Somireddy) · Updated 29 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open King Six and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete my account and data and confirm.

This permanently deletes your King Six account and everything stored in the cloud (teams, players, matches, balls, shares and your subscription record link), and erases the data on that phone. Guest sessions are deleted the same way.

By email

Cannot open the app? Email Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com from the Google account you used, with the subject "King Six data deletion request". We delete the account and data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Important: your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > King Six, or you may continue to be billed.

What we may keep

Records Google Play and tax rules require us to keep about payments may be retained by Google and, in minimal form (purchase token and dates, not linked to your cricket data), by us for fraud prevention and accounting.
