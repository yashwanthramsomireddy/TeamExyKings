Privacy Policy

King System App Remover · TeamExyKings · Last updated 12 November 2026

King System App Remover does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server. Everything the app shows is read from your device and stays on your device.

1. Information the app reads

To do its job the app reads the following from Android, each time you open or refresh it:

  • The list of apps installed on your device — name, package name, version, size, install and update dates, APK path, and whether the app is a system app or disabled.
  • If you have granted optional usage access: the date each app was last opened, and the app, data and cache sizes Android reports.

This information is held in memory only while the app is running. It is never written to a file, never backed up to a cloud service, and never sent anywhere.

2. Information the app stores on your device

  • Your own settings — sort order, which details to show in the list, and similar preferences.
  • A running total of bytes freed and a count of apps you have uninstalled through the app. No app names are kept.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall the app. You can clear the storage counter at any time from Settings.

3. Permissions and why they are needed

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES — the core purpose of the app is to list and uninstall your installed apps, which Android only permits with this declaration. It is used for nothing else.
  • REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES — allows the app to pass a package to Android's own uninstall dialog. The app cannot remove anything on its own; you confirm every removal in a system dialog.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (usage access) — entirely optional and granted by you in your phone's own settings. It is used only to display last-used dates and accurate app sizes. The app works without it. You can revoke it at any time in Settings → Apps → Special app access → Usage access.

4. No internet use

The app does not send your data to any server. Links to this website, the Play Store listing or the support email address open in another app you choose; what happens after that is governed by that app's and that service's own policies.

5. Children

The app is a general-purpose utility, is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's changelog.
