Delete your King Log data

by TeamExyKings · Last updated 29 October 2026 · applies to King Log 1.3 and newer

In short: King Log has no accounts and no servers, so there is no King Log account to close and nothing of yours on our side. Your data exists only on your phone, in the backups you made, and (if you connected it) in the KingLog folder in your own Google Drive. You can delete every copy yourself, in a minute, with the steps below. If you want us to confirm in writing that we hold nothing, email us.

1. Delete the data on your phone

  • In the app: Settings ▸ Your data ▸ Erase all data on this phone. This removes every Vault entry, Finance month, EMI plan, vehicle and fill-up.
  • Or in Android: Settings ▸ Apps ▸ King Log ▸ Storage ▸ Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

2. Delete your backups

  • On the phone: the weekly backups are in the Documents/KingLog folder. Delete that folder with your Files app. Some phones remove it together with the app's data, others keep it, so check.
  • A folder you chose (for example on an SD card or in a cloud-synced folder): delete the KingLog folder inside it. Settings ▸ Backup & restore also lets King Log forget the folder.
  • Files you saved yourself (Fuel or EMI exports, the recovery key file): they are ordinary files in the place you chose, usually Downloads. Delete them with your Files app.

3. Delete the copy in Google Drive

  • In the app: Settings ▸ Backup & restore ▸ Delete Drive backups. This deletes the KingLog folder that King Log created in your Drive and everything in it. Nothing else in your Drive is touched, because King Log cannot even see your other files.
  • Or in Drive: open drive.google.com and move the KingLog folder to the bin, then empty the bin.
  • Remove King Log's access: myaccount.google.com/permissions ▸ King Log ▸ Remove access.

4. Ask us to confirm

We run no server and receive no data from the app, so there is nothing for us to delete. If you would like that confirmed in writing, or you have written to us before and want that email thread deleted, use Settings ▸ Privacy & data ▸ Request data deletion in the app, or email yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com with the subject KingLog Data deletion request. We reply within 30 days. We never ask for your Vault, bank or card details; please do not send them.

5. What happens to your recovery key

The key lives only on your phone (and in the copy you saved). Deleting your data removes the key from the phone. Encrypted backups that you keep can then be opened only with the key file you saved. If you delete both the backups and the key file, nothing is left that anyone could read.
