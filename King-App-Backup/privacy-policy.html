Privacy Policy

King App Backup · TeamExyKings · Last updated 12 November 2026

In short: King App Backup collects nothing, sends nothing and stores nothing about you. It has no internet permission, no account and no analytics. Every file it writes stays in the folder you chose, on your own device.

1. Who this policy is from

King App Backup ("the app") is published by TeamExyKings, an independent developer trading as Yashwanth Ram Somireddy. This policy explains how the app handles information. Contact details are at the end.

2. Information the app collects

None. The app has no servers, no account system and no analytics or crash-reporting service. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device information.

3. Information the app reads on your device

To do its job, the app reads the following locally. None of it is recorded, uploaded or kept anywhere outside your device:

  • The list of apps installed on your device — name, package name, version, size, last-updated date and the location of each app's installer file. This is read from Android's own PackageManager each time you open or refresh the list, and is needed to show you what you can back up.
  • The backup folder you choose — the app can read and write only inside the single folder you grant it through Android's folder picker. It cannot see anything else in your storage.
  • Your settings — theme, sorting, and the checkboxes on the Settings screen are saved locally on the device.

4. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Query all packages — listing the apps you can back up is the entire purpose of the app, so it needs to see which apps are installed. Under Google Play's policy this is declared for the permitted "backup and restore" use.
  • Request install packages — needed so you can reinstall an archived APK from the Archived tab. Android still shows its own confirmation every time, and you can withdraw this permission in system settings at any time.

The app does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or broad storage access.

5. Files the app creates

In the folder you choose, the app writes APK files (and .apks archives for apps delivered as App Bundles), plus a small king_app_backup_index.json file that records each backup's app name, package name and version so the Archived tab can label them properly. These files belong to you. You can move, copy or delete them at any time, and uninstalling the app does not delete them.

6. Children

The app is a general-purpose utility, is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone.

7. Third parties

The app bundles no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries. It uses only Google's AndroidX and Jetpack libraries, which run locally and send nothing anywhere. If you tap a link in the app, your browser opens it and that site's own policy then applies.

8. Your rights

Because no data is collected, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase. All app data lives on your device: clearing the app's storage resets its settings, and deleting the files in your backup folder removes your backups.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's changelog.
