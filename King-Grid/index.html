What it does

Ten layouts

3×3, 2×2, 2×3 and more. Pick a layout and the preview shows exactly how the photo will be cut.

Numbered in posting order

Every tile is numbered in the preview and in its filename, so you never lose track of which one goes up next.

Carousel or feed grid

Carousel numbers left to right. Feed grid reverses the order so posting 1, 2, 3… rebuilds the picture on your profile.

Full resolution

Tiles are cut from the original file, not from a preview, so a 12 MP photo gives crisp 1200 px tiles.

Straight to your gallery

Tiles land in Pictures/KingGrid, each split in its own dated folder, ready to share from any app.

No permissions needed

King Grid uses the system photo picker and the system gallery, so it never asks for access to your photo library.

How it works

  1. Pick a photo. Any photo on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Choose a layout. The preview numbers each tile the way you will post it.
  3. Save the tiles. All of them go to your gallery in one tap, in order.

Questions

Why is part of my photo cut off?

With Square tiles on, the photo is centre-cropped so every tile is a perfect square, which is what a profile grid needs. Turn Square tiles off to use the whole photo.

Carousel or feed grid — which do I need?

Carousel for a multi-image post. Feed grid for a profile grid: a profile fills newest first, so the numbering is reversed and posting 1 to 9 assembles the picture correctly.

Are the numbers printed on the images?

No. The numbers appear only in the preview. Saved tiles are clean; the number lives in the filename.

Is anything uploaded?

Never. There is no account, no analytics and no network code. Photos are read, split and saved entirely on your device.
