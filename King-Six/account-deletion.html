King Six — Delete your account and data

App by TeamExyKings (Yashwanth Ram Somireddy) · Updated 11 November 2026

Delete inside the app

Open Settings > Delete my data and confirm. This permanently deletes your King Six account on our server (your email and your subscription record link) and erases all teams, players and matches from that phone.

Cannot open the app?

Email Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject King Six data deletion request. We will delete your account and reply when it is done.

Your data on the phone

Your teams, players and matches are stored only on your phone, so uninstalling the app also removes them.

What is not deleted

Backup files and Excel files you saved yourself, for example on Google Drive or your phone, are yours and are not touched by King Six. Delete them yourself if you no longer want them.

Important: your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > King Six, or you may continue to be billed.

What we may keep

Records Google Play and tax rules require us to keep about payments may be retained by Google and, in minimal form (purchase token and dates, not linked to your cricket data), by us for fraud prevention and accounting.
