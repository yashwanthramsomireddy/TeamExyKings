Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · DreamLoop 3.1
Zero tolerance. DreamLoop prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and sexualised content involving minors. We block it, remove it and report it.

1. How DreamLoop is built to prevent harm

  • The journal is private and stays on the user’s phone. DreamLoop is not a social network.
  • There is no public feed, no profiles, no direct messages, no comments, no image or video sharing and no way for users to find or contact each other.
  • Dream text leaves the phone only when the user taps an AI button (sent to our AI providers to produce the result, and not stored) or saves their own encrypted backup.
  • The app asks users to confirm they are 13 or older before continuing.

2. Our commitment

  • We do not allow DreamLoop to be used to create, store or distribute child sexual abuse material or to sexualise or exploit minors.
  • We review every report we receive and act on it, including suspending accounts that misuse the service.
  • We keep the app free of features that let strangers interact. If we ever add one, we will first add age checks, reporting and moderation, and update this page.

4. If we find CSAM

We remove it immediately, stop the account’s access, preserve the information the law requires, and report it to the appropriate authorities — in India through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and, where relevant, to law enforcement and child-protection bodies elsewhere.

5. Legal compliance

We comply with applicable child-safety laws, including India’s POCSO Act and IT Act reporting duties, and with Google Play’s Child Safety Standards policy.
