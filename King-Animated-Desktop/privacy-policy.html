Privacy Policy

King Animated Desktop · Last updated 31 October 2026 · applies to version 1.0.x · GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) & ePrivacy information

In short: the app itself collects, sends, sells or shares no personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising or cookies and makes no internet connections. Everything stays on your computer. The only time any personal data reaches us is if you choose to donate or email us (sections 6 and 7).

1. Who is responsible

TeamExyKings, Chennai, India (the “Publisher”). Privacy contact: Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com. Because the program sends nothing to the Publisher, there is no processing of your data by the Publisher through the app; this policy is provided for transparency and for the optional contacts below.

2. What the app stores on your computer (only on your device)

ItemWhereWhy
Your settings: chosen objects, custom texts you write, colours, sizes, badge text%APPDATA%\KingAnimatedDesktop\config.jsonSo your setup is remembered. Anything you type as text stays in this file only.
Start-up entry (only if “Start with Windows” is on)Registry: HKCU\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\RunTo start the program when you sign in.
Lock file kingdesktop.lockWindows temp folderPrevents two copies running at once.
Settings files you export yourselfWhere you save themBackup / moving to another PC.

None of this is transmitted anywhere.

3. What the app reads locally (never stored or sent)

  • Size and window class of the foreground window — only to hide the objects during full-screen apps if you turn on that option.
  • Power status (battery / mains) — only if you turn on “Hide when running on battery”.
  • The system clock — only if you turn on quiet hours.
  • Your monitor layout — to place objects on screen.

The program does not read your files, keystrokes, clipboard, other programs’ contents or screen contents. It draws small transparent windows above your screen, nothing more.

4. Legal basis, recipients and transfers

No personal data is processed through the app, so no consent is needed (Art. 6(1)(a)) and no cookies or similar technologies are used (ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC, Art. 5(3)). There are no recipients, processors or transfers outside the EU/EEA for the app itself. For the optional contacts below the legal bases are stated in each section.

5. Links that open your browser

The Info tab has buttons (website, source code, policies, donate, e-mail support). They only do something when you click them, and then your own browser or e-mail program opens the page. The app itself makes no connection. What those sites do with your visit is governed by their own policies: GitHub (code & downloads), teamexykings.in, PayPal, Razorpay.

These policy pages are bundled with the installer and open offline from your PC.

6. Donations (optional)

Donating is entirely voluntary and unlocks nothing. Clicking a donate button opens a payment page run by PayPal (PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A., Luxembourg, and PayPal Inc.) or Razorpay (Razorpay Software Private Limited, India). We never see or store your card, bank or UPI details.

  • What we receive: from the payment provider we receive the amount, date, transaction reference and the name and e-mail address you gave them (if shown to us). We use it only to confirm and acknowledge the donation, keep accounting records and meet legal obligations.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in handling donations you chose to make (Art. 6(1)(f)) and legal obligation for accounting (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Retention: as long as accounting / tax rules in India require (generally several years), then deleted.
  • Transfers: the providers may process data outside the EU/EEA (for example in India or the USA) under their own safeguards. See PayPal’s and Razorpay’s privacy policies.

7. If you contact us

If you e-mail Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com (for example via “Email support”), we receive your address and message and use them only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep it while the conversation is open and a reasonable time after, then delete it. Your mail provider also processes it under its own policy.

8. Websites & downloads

These pages and the downloads are hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages / Releases). GitHub may log technical visitor data such as your IP address under its own policy. These pages use no cookies, no analytics, no external fonts or scripts; the only thing stored in your browser is your light/dark choice (local storage), which never leaves your device.

9. Retention and erasure (right to be forgotten)

Your app data lives only on your PC. Delete it any time: uninstall and answer Yes to “Also delete my settings?”, or delete the folder %APPDATA%\KingAnimatedDesktop. Turning off “Start with Windows” in Settings removes the start-up entry.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your national data-protection authority (list: edpb.europa.eu). For app data you exercise these directly on your own device as described above; for donation or e-mail records, write to Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

11. Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. Donating is intended for adults.

12. Security

The program has no network functionality and no server component and does not need administrator rights after installation. Download it only from the official Releases page and check the file hash if provided.

13. Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page and CHANGELOG.md will be updated before release. Current version of this policy: 31 October 2026.

This policy is provided in good faith for an open-source hobby project; it is not legal advice.
