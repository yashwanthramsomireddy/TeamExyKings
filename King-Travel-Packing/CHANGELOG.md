# Changelog

All notable changes to King Travel are recorded here.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-12

First release.

### Added
- Multiple trips, each with its own checklist, destination, start date and notes.
- Eight starting points: Blank, Weekend getaway, Beach holiday, Business trip, International trip, Family trip, Trek & outdoors, Pilgrimage.
- Items grouped into Essentials, Documents, Clothing, Toiletries, Electronics, Health and Extras, with an optional quantity.
- Tap-to-tick packing with a live progress bar, per-category counts and a packed / left / total summary.
- Reset ticks to reuse a checklist for the next trip, mark all packed, and remove packed items.
- Duplicate a trip (copied unticked), archive and restore trips.
- Add extra items into an existing trip from any template, skipping anything already on the list.
- Add, edit and delete your own items.
- Share a checklist as plain text.
- Export all trips to a JSON backup and restore it by merging or replacing.
- Settings: theme (Pitch black, Dark, Light, Follow system), hide packed items, progress on trip cards, confirm before deleting, keep screen on, delete all trips.
- About screen with version, credits, website, privacy policy, terms of use, support email and share.
- FAQ screen with search, grouped by topic.
- Pitch black AMOLED theme with a white accent, and a splash screen.
