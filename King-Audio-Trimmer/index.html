King Audio Trimmer

Trim, cut and merge audio on your phone. Free forever. Works offline.

What it does

  • Pick any audio file (or the sound of a video) with Android's own file picker
  • See the waveform, drag the start and end handles, pinch to zoom, or type exact times
  • Keep a selection or remove a part from the middle, with undo and redo
  • Fade in and out, change volume, normalize, change speed, reverse, trim silence
  • Merge several songs into one with a gap or a crossfade
  • Ringtone maker for ringtones, notifications and alarms
  • Save as M4A (AAC), AAC, Opus, FLAC or WAV, or in the original format
  • Lossless cuts for MP3, M4A/AAC and Opus files: no re-encoding, no quality loss

Private by design

  • No ads, no subscription, no account, no analytics, no tracking
  • No storage permission needed
  • Your audio never leaves your phone

Make it yours

  • Pitch Black (default), White or System theme
  • Five font choices and an accent colour picker
  • Backup and restore your settings to a single file
