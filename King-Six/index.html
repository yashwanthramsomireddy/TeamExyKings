King Six

Score it. Share it. Own it.

A ball-by-ball cricket scorer for backyard, club and street cricket, by TeamExyKings. Your teams, players and scores are stored on your phone. No ads, no tracking.

  • Ball-by-ball scoring with extras, wickets, free hits, retirements, penalties and super overs
  • Scorecards, worm and run-rate graphs, player and team stats, series tables
  • Free for everyone: create teams, add players and score matches
  • Share a scorecard as text, image, PDF or CSV
  • King Six Pro (yearly): back up to Google Drive or your phone's storage, and export your whole profile to Excel
