4. Accuracy of readings

Every reading comes from the sensors in your own device. Their accuracy depends on the hardware, on Android's calibration of it, and on the conditions around you. In particular:

  • Headings are magnetic north, not true north, and are not corrected for local magnetic declination
  • Magnetic readings are distorted by metal, magnets, speakers, vehicles, electronics and reinforced structures
  • Heading accuracy falls away as the phone is tilted off flat
  • Level and slope readings depend on the device and its case sitting flat against the surface
  • Some devices have no magnetometer at all, in which case no heading is available

Readings are provided for general information only. Verify anything that matters with a purpose-built instrument.
