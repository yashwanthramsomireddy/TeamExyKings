Privacy Policy

King Audio Trimmer · TeamExyKings · Last updated: 11 November 2026

King Audio Trimmer ("the app") is built by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy under the TeamExyKings brand. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no tracking of any kind. We do not receive any information about you or your audio files.

Your audio files

You choose files with Android's system file picker. The app reads them only to show the waveform, play your selection and create the trimmed copy you ask for. All processing happens on your phone. Audio is never uploaded or shared. Your original files are never modified.

Finished files are saved on your phone using Android's media library: in the Music folder (inside a "King Audio Trimmer" folder), or in the Ringtones, Notifications or Alarms folder if you choose that. While working, the app keeps temporary files in its private cache on your phone, plus a small cached waveform for files you have opened. These are removed automatically or when you clear the app's cache.

Permissions

The app does not request storage, microphone, contacts, location or any other permission, and it does not declare internet access.

Data stored on your phone

The app stores your settings (theme, font, accent colour, default fade length) and a short list of recently opened files (file name, length and a reference to the file) on your device only. You can clear the recent list from the home screen. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Backup and restore

If you use Backup in Settings, the app writes one JSON file containing your settings and recent-files list to a location you choose. We never receive this file. Audio is not included in backups.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
