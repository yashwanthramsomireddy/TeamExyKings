Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · DreamLoop 3.1
In short. Your dreams live on your phone. We never see them unless you tap an AI button or choose to share one to Community. Backups are encrypted with a passphrase only you know. We don’t sell data and show no ads.

1. Who we are

DreamLoop is made by TeamExyKings (developer: Yash). Privacy questions: support@teamexykings.in or yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com.

3. What we never do

  • Read, store or train models on your private dreams.
  • Sell or share your data for advertising. There are no ads and no trackers.
  • Write dream text to server logs.

4. Community sharing (optional)

  • Off by default. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share on a specific dream, confirm you are 18 or older, and it passes our checks.
  • What others see: title, story, mood and month only. No name, email, account ID or exact date. Plain text only — no images or links.
  • Before anything is shown it is checked automatically (on your phone, then on our server with an AI safety check). Dreams with adult, hateful, self-harm or personal-detail content are not published and stay private.
  • Anyone signed in can report a dream. Three reports hide it until we review it. Repeated violations switch sharing off for that account.
  • Remove a shared dream any time: Settings → My shared dreams. Deleting your account deletes everything you shared.
  • Once public, a dream can be seen or copied by anyone who viewed it. Share only what you are comfortable with.

5. Backups and security

  • Backups are encrypted on your phone (AES-256-GCM, key derived from your passphrase with scrypt) before upload or saving. We and Google see only scrambled data.
  • We cannot recover your passphrase. If you forget it, that backup cannot be opened.
  • The passphrase is stored only if you choose “Remember on this phone”, in your phone’s secure keystore.
  • Screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the app.
  • Drive access uses the limited drive.file permission: the app can only see files it created.
  • Excel export is a readable file for your own use and is not encrypted.
  • All connections use HTTPS. Credit balances can only be changed by our server.

6. Service providers

Supabase (accounts, credits, shared dreams) · Google (sign-in, Drive, Play Billing) · OpenRouter and the AI model providers it routes to (AI text, safety check) · Brevo (optional purchase receipt e-mail). Each gets only what its function needs.

7. Keeping and deleting data

  • Dreams stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This erases your account, credits, purchase records and shared dreams from our servers and your dreams from the phone. Your own Drive backups stay in your Drive until you delete them.
  • Records that the law requires (for example payment records held by Google) are kept by those providers.

8. Children

DreamLoop is for people aged 13 and over. Community sharing is for adults (18+) only, and content involving minors in any sexual or unsafe way is blocked and reported. See Child safety.

9. Your rights

You can see, export and delete your data in the app. Where the law gives you more (for example India’s DPDP Act or GDPR), write to us and we will help.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
