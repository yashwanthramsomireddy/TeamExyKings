Your secrets, your money and your miles.
One private app.

KingLog keeps your banking, card, ID, WiFi and password vaults, your salary and savings, and your bike and car fuel, behind a PIN or pattern and fingerprint lock. Your data stays on your phone. No ads, no tracking.

View on GitHub Privacy policy

Version 1.0.0, in testing. Google Play release coming soon.

Vault

Four private vaults, encrypted on your phone.

  • Banking, Cards, IDs & WiFi and Passwords tabs
  • Entries encrypted with a key kept in Android Keystore
  • Secrets hidden, copy auto-clears, no screenshots
  • Import your old vault JSON files
  • Optional Drive backup encrypted with your passphrase

Finance

Know exactly what lands in the bank and what you keep.

  • Simple and Pro salary calculators
  • Gratuity, PVL per day and PI helpers
  • Monthly snapshots and savings rate
  • Analytics: bars, trends and spend donut
  • Indian ₹ formatting, export to JSON / MD / HTML

Fuel Log

Bikes and cars, every fill-up, real mileage.

  • Add bikes and cars, log fill-ups in seconds
  • Full-to-full mileage, cost per km, ₹ per litre
  • Monthly spend, mileage and cost trends
  • Filter by bikes, cars or a single vehicle
  • Import and export CSV

Built to be trusted

Simple promises, written down in the privacy policy.

On your phone

All entries live in the app's private storage. No KingLog account or server.

PIN, pattern, biometrics

6-digit PIN or a 3×3 pattern, fingerprint / face unlock, auto-lock and lock-out after wrong tries.

Your backup, your place

Back up to a hidden KingLog-only folder in your own Google Drive, or to any folder on your phone. Sign-in is optional.

Make it yours

Pitch black by default with a white accent. Choose Black, Grey, White, System or Custom, and any accent colour.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics, no ad SDKs, no location. Only two permissions: internet and biometrics.

Open source

Read the code and check the claims on GitHub.

Honest note

Vault entries are encrypted on the phone and the Vault Drive backup uses your passphrase (we can't recover it). Finance and Fuel Drive backups are protected by your Google account only; KingLog does not add its own encryption to those yet.
