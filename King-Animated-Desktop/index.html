King Animated Desktop

Little animated friends and your own custom text, floating over every app on your screen: a web-swinging hero, a trotting Labrador, a neon badge, 61 objects to mix and match — and your own text with any position, animation or swing.

Download for WindowsView source on GitHub

What you get

61 animated objects

Animals, birds, characters, flyers, floaters, fallers, sports balls and corner decor. Tick the ones you like or use a preset.

Custom text

Write anything: pick a font, colour and box style, put it in any corner or exact spot, animate it (16 animations) or hang it from a thread and let it swing.

Over all your apps

Click-through, always-on-top overlays that stay above your windows without stealing focus or blocking your mouse. Multi-monitor aware.

68 global + per-object settings

Size, speed, colours, hue, rainbow, swing physics, quiet hours, battery & full-screen rules, autostart and more. Live preview.

Pitch-black & white themes

A tidy settings window in pitch black (default), white or system theme, with your own accent colour.

Private by design

No accounts, no telemetry, no network connections. Settings stay on your PC. Read the privacy policy.

Install

  1. Download KingAnimatedDesktop_Setup_1.0.0.exe from the Releases page.
  2. Accept the licence, choose the options (desktop shortcut, start with Windows) and finish. It installs to C:\Program Files\King Animated Desktop.
  3. Right-click the tray icon (near the clock) for Settings, Pause and Quit.
Windows may show a SmartScreen warning for new, unsigned open-source programs. Choose More info → Run anyway only if you downloaded it from the official Releases page above. Exclusive full-screen games (DirectX/Vulkan exclusive mode) cannot be drawn over by any normal window; borderless/windowed games work.

Questions

Does it slow my PC down?

A handful of objects use very little CPU. You can lower the frame rate, cap the number of objects, hide on battery or hide during full-screen apps.

How do I remove it?

Windows Settings → Apps → King Animated Desktop → Uninstall. You will be asked whether to delete your settings too.

Can I add my own objects?

Yes — it is open source. See CONTRIBUTING.md.
