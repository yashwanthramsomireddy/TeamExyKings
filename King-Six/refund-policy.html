King Six — Refund and Cancellation Policy

App by TeamExyKings (Yashwanth Ram Somireddy) · Updated 11 November 2026

How billing works

King Six Pro is a yearly subscription billed by Google Play to your Google Play account. It renews automatically every year until you cancel. The price is shown in Google Play before you buy and next to the Upgrade button in the app.

Cancelling

Cancel any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > King Six. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app or your King Six account does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Google, so refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. You can request a refund through Google Play; Google decides eligibility. If you have a problem with the app itself, email us and we will help.

When Pro ends

After a paid year ends, Pro keeps working for 7 more days (grace week) so you can renew. After that your account goes back to the Free plan: you keep your data on the phone and can keep scoring, but Drive backups and the Excel export are locked until you renew.
