King Parking

Never lose your car again. Save the spot, walk straight back to it.

Free forever No ads No tracking Fully offline No sign-in

What it does

Saves the exact position

One tap stores the GPS coordinates of your vehicle, with the accuracy of the fix shown so you know what to expect.

Bay photo and note

Snap the pillar number or the row you parked in and add a note. GPS cannot tell you which floor you are on — a photo can.

Walk back

Hands the position to your maps app with walking directions, or just drops a pin so you can see where it is.

More than one vehicle

Save as many spots as you like — the car, the bike, a friend's vehicle. Each one is listed with how long it has been parked.

History

Cleared spots move to a history list, so a car park you use often is one tap away from being saved again.

Backup in your hands

Write a single JSON file whenever you want and restore it on another phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How it works

  1. Park, open King Parking and tap Save parking spot.
  2. Wait a moment for the GPS fix, then add a level or bay, a note and a photo if you want them.
  3. When you come back, open the spot and tap Walk back to my vehicle.
  4. Tap I found my vehicle to clear it.

Privacy in one line

King Parking ships without the internet permission. Your positions, photos and notes stay in the app's private storage on your phone, and nothing is ever collected, uploaded or shared.

Permissions it asks for

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a bay photo. Photos are stored privately and never appear in your gallery.
