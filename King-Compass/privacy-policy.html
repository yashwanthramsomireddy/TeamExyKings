Privacy Policy

King Compass · com.teamexykings.kingcompass
Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Effective: 12 November 2026

King Compass collects nothing, stores nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere. The app has no internet permission, so it could not transmit data even if it tried.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to the King Compass Android application, published by TeamExyKings (Yashwanth Ram Somireddy), Chennai, India — referred to below as "we" or "the developer".

2. Information we collect

None. King Compass does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details — there is no sign-in
  • Your location, whether precise or approximate
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or installed-app lists
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, telemetry or diagnostics
  • Photos, files, contacts, calendar entries or messages

3. Sensor data

King Compass reads your device's magnetometer, accelerometer and the Android fused rotation-vector sensor to produce the heading, tilt and slope readings. This data is processed in memory, on your device, for as long as the reading is on screen. It is never recorded, stored or transmitted. Motion sensors require no runtime permission on Android.

4. Settings stored on your device

Your preferences — dial behaviour, smoothing, haptics, keep-screen-on, and any level calibration offset you set — are saved in the app's own private storage on the device, using Android's DataStore. These values never leave the phone. Uninstalling the app removes them. "Reset all settings" inside the app clears them immediately.

If you have Android's own backup enabled for your Google account, these preference values may be included in that device backup. That backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

5. Permissions

  • VIBRATE — produces the short haptic tick when the heading crosses a cardinal point or the bubble centres. It can be turned off in Settings, and it grants no access to any data.

King Compass requests no other permission. It has no INTERNET permission, no location permission, no storage permission and no camera permission.

6. Third parties

King Compass contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no social or login SDK. No third party receives data from this app.

The app contains links to our website, this policy, the terms of use, the Google Play listing, and an email address for support. Opening any of them hands you over to your browser, email or Play Store app, which have their own privacy policies. Sending us a support email means we receive your email address and whatever you write; we use it only to reply to you.

7. Children

King Compass is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

8. Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or held, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. Everything the app stores sits in its own sandbox on your device and is under your control: clear it with "Reset all settings", with Android's Clear data, or by uninstalling.

9. Security

All processing happens locally on your device within the app's private storage sandbox, which Android isolates from other apps. No data is transmitted, so there is no transmission to intercept.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date, and a material change will be noted in the app's release notes. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
