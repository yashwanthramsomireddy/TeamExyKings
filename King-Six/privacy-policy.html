King Six — Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 (app version 1.6.0) · App by TeamExyKings (Yashwanth Ram Somireddy)

The short version

King Six requires a Google sign-in and an internet connection. Your teams, players and scores are saved to your own King Six account in the cloud so they are backed up and follow you to a new phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account: your Google account's email address and a unique account id, provided by Google Sign-In.
  • Cricket data you enter: team names, player names and nicknames, and match scores. You confirm you have each player's permission before adding them.
  • Subscription record: the purchase token and dates Google Play provides when you subscribe to King Six Pro, used to confirm your plan, and a note of each paid period so we can see renewals and total subscription income. We never see your card or payment details.

We do not collect your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or advertising identifiers.

What the owner of King Six can see

To run the service and support you, the owner of King Six (Yashwanth Ram Somireddy) can see, through a private admin screen protected by a server-side check, a list of accounts with their email address, display name, join and last sign-in dates, plan (Free, Pro, grace or expired) and subscription dates. The owner can also send a renewal reminder to accounts whose Pro year has ended or is about to end. The owner does not read your teams, players or matches in that screen.

Notifications

King Six can show reminders on your phone: Pro renewal reminders (daily in the last 7 days of your paid year and during the 7-day grace period after it), a monthly reminder to save a backup copy, and a renewal reminder sent by the owner if you have not renewed. These are generated on your phone from your plan dates; no push-messaging service is used and nothing is sent to your email. Android asks for permission first, and you can switch each kind off in Settings > Reminders or in Android's notification settings.

Backups you keep

King Six can create a backup file of your teams, players and matches. Once a month it saves a copy on your phone (inside the app, or in a folder you choose). You can also save a copy to Google Drive or anywhere else using Android's file picker. King Six does not connect to your Google Drive, cannot see your Drive, and only writes the file you ask it to write; Google's own privacy policy applies to files you place in Drive. Deleting your King Six account does not touch backup files you saved elsewhere.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Your data is stored on your phone and in a cloud database and authentication service provided by Supabase. Each account can read only its own data. If you create a match code, anyone who has that code and a King Six account can view that single match until you stop sharing it.

Sign-in and subscriptions

Sign-in is provided by Google. Pro is an optional yearly subscription bought and managed in Google Play; Google handles your payment details and King Six never sees your card. The app sends the Google Play purchase token to our server, which verifies it with Google Play to confirm your plan. A hashed form of your account id is attached to the purchase so it cannot be used on another account.

Sharing

When you tap Share on a scorecard, King Six creates a text, image, PDF or file and hands it to the Android share sheet; you choose where it goes and that app's own privacy policy applies from then on.

Guest mode

You can continue as a guest. A guest session is an anonymous account with no email address; it is view-only and can be deleted in the app.

Fonts and tracking

The Nunito font is bundled inside the app (SIL Open Font License); no font is downloaded from anyone. King Six has no analytics, advertising, session recording, keystroke logging or crash-reporting service.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. In the app, Settings > Delete my data permanently deletes your account and all of your data in the cloud and erases the data on your phone. It does not touch backup files you saved to Google Drive or elsewhere. You can also email us to request deletion. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. Signing out removes the data from your phone only; it remains in your account. Subscription and payment records (token, dates and amount, not linked to your cricket data) may be kept as required for accounting and fraud prevention.

Children

King Six is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. You confirm you are 13 or older when you sign in; where the law sets a higher age, a parent or guardian must agree. Add a player only with their permission (or a parent or guardian's for a child). If you believe a child's data was added by mistake, email us and we will delete it.
