Free and Pro

Everyone can create teams, add players and series, score matches, see all graphs, player stats and leaderboards, and share scorecards as text, image, PDF or CSV. Subscriptions and billing

Pro is billed yearly through Google Play, renews automatically until you cancel, and can be managed or cancelled any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. Refunds follow Google Play's policy. If your yearly subscription ends, Pro keeps working for 7 more days (grace week) so you can renew. After that your account goes back to the Free plan: you can keep scoring, but Drive backups and the Excel export are locked until you renew. Your data is not deleted when Pro ends.
