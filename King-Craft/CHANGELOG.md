- Stability: guards against the app showing a blank black screen after returning from the background (fresh redraw on resume, window colour follows the theme, picture memory freed when the app is hidden). The last crash, if any, is kept on the phone and offered in the Support email.
- Home: Info (About) and ? (FAQ) icons next to Settings. About and FAQ are still in Settings too.
- Password boxes (Lock, Unlock, Extract text, anywhere a PDF password is typed) now have a show / hide eye.
- About links now use the King-Craft website folder: Visit Website opens teamexykings.in, Privacy and Terms open teamexykings.in/King-Craft/.
