Privacy Policy

King Grid · TeamExyKings · Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version: King Grid collects nothing, sends nothing and stores nothing about you. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network access. Every photo you open stays on your device.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to the King Grid Android application ("the app"), published by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy under the TeamExyKings brand.

2. Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Specifically, it does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details — there is no sign-in.
  • Your photos or any part of them.
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or location.
  • Usage analytics, crash telemetry or diagnostics.

3. How your photos are handled

  • You choose a photo through the Android system photo picker. The app receives only the single photo you select; it is not given access to the rest of your gallery.
  • The photo is read, split into tiles and written back to your device's gallery. All of this happens locally, on your phone.
  • The app keeps no copy of the photo and no record of which photos you have used.
  • Tiles are saved to Pictures/KingGrid. From that point they are ordinary files in your gallery, under your control.

4. Permissions

  • On Android 10 and later, the app requests no permissions at all. The system photo picker and the system gallery handle both reading and writing.
  • On Android 9 and earlier, the app declares the storage write permission, which older versions of Android require in order to save an image into the gallery. It is used for nothing else.
  • The app does not declare the internet permission, so it is not technically capable of sending anything anywhere.

5. Settings stored on your device

Your preferences — default layout, output format, quality, numbering and toggles — are stored locally on your device so the app remembers them. They never leave the device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

6. Third parties

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics software development kits and no third-party trackers. If you tap Share, the app you share into is governed by its own privacy policy, not this one.

7. Children

King Grid is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
