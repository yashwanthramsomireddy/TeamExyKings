Privacy Policy

King Travel (com.teamexykings.kingtravelcheck) · TeamExyKings · Last updated 12 November 2026

Short version: King Travel does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Everything you type stays on your device.

1. Who we are

King Travel is developed and published by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy under the brand TeamExyKings. For anything in this policy you can write to Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

None. King Travel has no account system, no sign-in and no server. We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or any other contact details
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or location data
  • Usage analytics, crash telemetry or behavioural data
  • Photos, files, contacts or anything else outside the app

3. Information stored on your device

Everything you create in the app — trip names, destinations, dates, checklist items, quantities, notes and your app settings — is written to the app's own private storage on your phone. It is readable only by King Travel, it is never uploaded, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Backups you create

The Settings screen can export your trips to a JSON file. You choose where that file is saved through Android's own file picker. Once saved, the file is under your control: we never see it, and anything you do with it afterwards (copying it to another phone, a cloud drive or a computer) is your choice and governed by whichever service you use.

5. Permissions

King Travel requests no Android runtime permissions. It does not declare the INTERNET permission, so the app cannot send data anywhere even if it wanted to.

6. Network use

The app itself does not make network requests. Links on the About screen (website, privacy policy, terms, support email, share) hand off to your browser, email app or share sheet. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

7. Third-party services

There are no advertising networks, analytics SDKs, crash reporters or tracking libraries in King Travel. The app is distributed through Google Play, and Google collects its own install and crash data under the Google Play Terms of Service; we have no access to anything that identifies you.

8. Children

King Travel is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Your rights

Because nothing leaves your device, there is no data for us to show, correct, export or erase on your behalf. You are in full control: edit or delete any trip at any time, use Delete all trips in Settings to wipe the app's data, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

10. Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox and by your device's own lock screen and encryption. We recommend keeping a device lock enabled and storing exported backups somewhere you trust.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

Questions, corrections or concerns: Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com
