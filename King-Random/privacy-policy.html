Privacy Policy

King Random · Effective 12 November 2026 · Last updated 12 November 2026

In short: King Random collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, no account system and no analytics. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your own device.

1. Who this policy is from

King Random (com.teamexykings.kingrandom) is an Android application published by TeamExyKings, an independent software project run by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

2. Information we collect

None. King Random does not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share any personal information. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail
  • Your location, whether precise or approximate
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or installed-app lists
  • Usage analytics, event logs, heat maps or session recordings
  • Crash reports sent to any third party
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data

3. Information stored on your device

So the app is useful between sessions, King Random saves the following in Android's own private app storage, readable only by King Random:

  • Your settings — theme, haptics, animations, keep-screen-on and history preferences
  • Your result history — the numbers, rolls, flips, spins and shuffles the app produced
  • Your spin wheels and their options
  • Your saved shuffle lists
  • Your coin-flip counts and streaks
  • The last range and dice settings you used, so the app opens where you left it

This data never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it yourself. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling King Random removes all of it permanently.

4. Internet access

King Random does not request the INTERNET permission. It is technically incapable of sending data anywhere. The app requests no runtime permissions of any kind.

5. Backup and restore

The Settings screen can write a single JSON backup file containing your history, wheels, saved lists, coin stats and settings. You choose where that file is saved through Android's own file picker, and you control it completely from then on. King Random does not upload it, read it back automatically, or have any knowledge of where you put it. Restoring simply reads a file you select.

If you have Android's system backup enabled on your device, Android itself may include the app's data in your own Google account backup. That is a function of your device and your Google account settings, governed by Google's privacy policy, not by TeamExyKings.

6. Third-party services

King Random contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no social media SDKs and no payment processing. No third party receives data from this app.

Links in the About screen (website, privacy policy, terms, Play Store listing) open in your browser or in the Play Store app. Once you follow a link, the privacy policy of that destination applies. The Support / Contact button opens your own email app with a message pre-filled; nothing is sent until you send it.

7. Children's privacy

King Random is suitable for general audiences and contains no advertising, no purchases and no user-generated content from anyone else. As the app collects no information whatsoever, it collects nothing from children either.

8. Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or held by TeamExyKings, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. All data lives on your device and is entirely under your control: clear it from the app's Settings screen, clear the app's storage from Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

9. Security

Your King Random data is protected by Android's application sandbox, which prevents other apps from reading it. A backup file you export is a plain, readable JSON file — store it somewhere you are comfortable with.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
