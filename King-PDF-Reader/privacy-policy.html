Privacy Policy

King PDF Reader · TeamExyKings · Last updated 12 November 2026

King PDF Reader does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it has no technical ability to send anything off your device.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to the Android application King PDF Reader (package com.teamexykings.kingpdfreader), published by TeamExyKings and developed by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy.

2. Information we collect

None. There is no account, no registration and no sign-in. We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or any other contact details
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or location data
  • Usage analytics, event logs or crash reports
  • The contents, names or metadata of the PDFs you open

3. What the app stores on your device

So that the app is useful across sessions, the following is saved in the app's own private storage on your device. It never leaves the device and is not readable by other apps.

  • Your settings — night mode, page layout, render quality and the other preferences on the Settings screen.
  • Recent files — for each document you open, the system reference to it, its file name and size, and the page you reached.
  • Bookmarks — the page numbers and labels you save inside documents.

All of it is removed when you uninstall the app. You can clear it at any time from Settings, using Clear recent files and Clear all bookmarks.

4. Your PDF files

When you pick a PDF, Android grants the app permission to read that one file. The app reads it in place to draw it on screen. Your PDFs are never copied, uploaded, modified or deleted by King PDF Reader.

5. Permissions

  • No internet permission. The app cannot make network requests.
  • No storage permission. Files are opened only through Android's own document picker, one file at a time, with your explicit choice.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts, location or any other sensitive permission is requested.

6. Links out of the app

The About screen can open this website, the Play Store listing and your mail app. Those open in a separate app of your choosing and are then governed by that app's and that site's own terms. Nothing about you is attached to those links.

7. Backups you create

Settings → Back up to file writes your settings, recent files list and bookmarks to a JSON file at a location you choose. That file is yours: where it goes and who can read it is under your control, not ours. If you save it to a cloud folder, that provider's privacy policy applies to it.

Android may also include the app's settings in your own device backup if you have that turned on in system settings. That backup belongs to your Google account, not to us.

8. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

9. Third-party services

The app bundles no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting service. PDF rendering uses Android's own built-in PDF engine, part of the operating system. Distribution through Google Play means Google collects its own installation and purchase statistics, governed by Google's privacy policy and not by us.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version is published on this page with a new date. Any change that affects what the app stores or sends will also be noted in the app's changelog.
