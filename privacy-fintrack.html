Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026
🤖 Android 💰 Free Forever · No Ads

Short version: FinTrack works fully offline in Guest mode — your salary and expense data never leaves your device unless you choose to sign in. If you sign in with Google, your data syncs to our database so it's available across your devices. We don't run ads, analytics, or sell your data. The donate button opens your UPI app directly — we never see your payment details. Google Drive backup is planned but not live yet; this page will be updated when it ships.

1. Information We Collect

We do not run ad tracking, analytics, or sell your data. What's collected depends entirely on how you use the app:

  • Guest mode (default): Nothing is collected by us. Your salary, expenses, and settings are stored only in local storage on your device.
  • Signed in with Google: Your Google account email and name (to create your account), plus the salary and expense entries you add, are stored in our database so you can access them across devices.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

In Guest mode, all data lives in local storage on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. If you sign in, your salary and expense entries are stored in our backend database (Supabase), encrypted in transit, and accessible only to your signed-in account. You can delete your data or your account at any time from within the app.

3. Third-Party Services

FinTrack uses the following third-party services, only for the features they support:

  • Google Sign-In — Used only if you choose to sign in instead of using Guest mode. Governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Supabase — Our backend provider for authentication and database storage, used only for signed-in accounts.
  • Your UPI app — The donate button opens your device's UPI payment app with an amount pre-filled, via a standard UPI deep link. The transaction is handled entirely by your UPI app and your bank; we never receive your payment or bank details.

Coming soon — Google Drive backup: we're planning an optional backup feature using Google's narrowest Drive scope (drive.file, access only to files FinTrack itself creates). It is not available in the app yet. This page will be updated with the exact details — including whether the backup is encrypted — once that feature ships, rather than describing it in advance.

4. Permissions Used

FinTrack requests internet access, used only when you sign in or sync data, and storage access, used only to save the files you export (JSON/Markdown/HTML) to your device. In Guest mode the app makes no network requests related to your financial data at all. FinTrack does not request access to your contacts, camera, SMS, or location.

5. Analytics & Monetization

FinTrack contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs of any kind. The app is free with no in-app purchases or subscriptions — the only payment-adjacent feature is the voluntary donate button described above.

6. Children's Privacy

FinTrack does not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and the app is not directed at children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of FinTrack after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
