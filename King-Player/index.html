King Player

A music and video player for everything already on your phone.

Free forever No ads No subscription No login No tracking
Get it on Google Play

Music

Your local library, played properly.

Everything on the device

MP3, FLAC, AAC, M4A, WAV, OGG and Opus, found automatically through Android's own media library.

Four-corner visualizer

Rainbow arcs sweep clockwise around every corner of Now Playing and pulse while the track runs.

Equalizer

Bass, mid and treble sliders with Normal, Rock, Pop, Jazz, Classical and Bass Boost presets.

Playlists

Create, rename and edit playlists. They stay on the device — no account, no sync, no cloud.

Keeps playing

Lock the screen or switch apps and the music carries on, with controls on the lock screen and in the notification.

Sleep timer

Pick ten minutes to an hour and a half and playback pauses itself.

Video

Full screen, gesture-first.

Broad format support

MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WebM and 3GP, with H.264, H.265/HEVC, VP9 and AV1 wherever the device decodes them.

Gestures

Brightness on the left half, volume on the right half, swipe sideways to seek, double-tap to skip ten seconds.

Subtitles

.srt, .ass, .ssa and .vtt. A subtitle file sitting next to the video loads on its own.

Aspect ratio

Switch between Fit, Fill and Zoom without leaving the player.

Picture-in-Picture

Leave the app and the video shrinks into a floating window that follows you around.

Audio keeps going

Lock the screen during a video and the sound carries on, with the same notification controls.

Privacy by construction

There is nothing to opt out of.

  • No network calls. The app never contacts a server. Not for ads, not for analytics, not for updates.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
  • No analytics or crash reporting. No third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • Your files stay yours. Media is read from the device and played. It is never copied, uploaded or indexed elsewhere.
  • The microphone permission is optional. Android requires it for the audio-reactive visualizer. That mode is off by default, the permission is only asked for if you turn it on, and nothing is ever recorded.
