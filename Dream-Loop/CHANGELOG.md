# Changelog

## [4.8.4] - 2026-11-11

### Added
- Font choice in Settings > Appearance: Dream (default), Clean, Classic, Rounded and Typewriter. Applies to the whole app.
- About (i) and FAQ & help (?) icons at the top of the Settings screen. The rows inside Settings stay as before.

### Changed
- The bottom bar no longer shows a grey highlight behind the selected tab. The tap animation is unchanged.

### Developer note
- New packages: @expo-google-fonts/inter, lora, nunito. Run `npm install` after copying the files.
