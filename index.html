About

Who we are

About TeamExyKings

TeamExyKings started as a Samsung Galaxy ROM & kernel development team around 2014 — building custom builds for the Galaxy S5 through S10 and Note10 era — before evolving into a broader developer collective building free tools and apps.

Our current tools are actively maintained. The ROM and kernel archive is preserved here for reference and is no longer actively developed.

GitHub ↗ Telegram ↗

Current Status

Our current tools — Bookmark Tab Manager, DebloatKit, PurgeKit, QuickOff, and more — are actively maintained and open source.

The ROM and kernel archive (2014–2020) is preserved for reference. No new ROM builds are planned.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is TeamExyKings?
A developer collective that started building custom ROMs and kernels for Samsung Galaxy devices, now focused on free tools and browser extensions.
Are the custom ROMs still being updated?
No — this work is archived. We're not actively developing new builds but preserve the history and links here for reference.
Which Samsung devices were supported?
Note, S, A and Tab series from roughly the Galaxy S5 through the S10 / Note 10 era. See the Legacy Archive for the full list.
Is it safe to flash these ROMs?
⚠️ Use at your own risk. TeamExyKings is not responsible for any damage to your device, data loss, or voided warranties resulting from flashing custom ROMs or kernels. Always maintain a full backup of your data and the original firmware before proceeding.
Custom ROMs and kernels are provided for educational and personal use only. Risks include bootloops, soft-bricks, or permanent device damage if performed incorrectly.
How do I report an issue with current tools?
Open an issue on the relevant GitHub repository — links are on each project card in the Projects section.
Where can I download ROM / kernel builds?
Links are being added device by device — check back, or watch the Telegram channel for updates.
