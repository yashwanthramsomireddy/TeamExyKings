Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026
🤖 Android 🌙 iOS

Short version: Your dream entries themselves are never sent to our servers — they stay on your device, and leave it only when you actively trigger an AI feature (sent to the AI provider for that one request) or turn on Google Drive backup (sent to your own Drive). Our backend only ever sees your account identity, credit balance, and transaction/AI-usage records — never your dream text. We don't run ads, analytics, or sell your data. Sign-in uses Google only.

1. Information We Collect

We do not run ad tracking or analytics, and we do not sell or share your data with advertisers. The following is collected or transmitted, only as needed to provide the app's features:

  • Your Google account email and name, when you sign in — used to create and identify your account
  • Your credit balance and purchase/transaction records, and an AI-usage log (which model was used, token counts, and cost estimate) — kept so we can operate the credits system and track our own AI costs. This log does not include your dream text.
  • Your dream entry text — sent to an AI model (via OpenRouter, see Section 3) only at the moment you tap an AI feature (recurring-pattern detection or the Nightmare Resolver) for that specific entry. Not stored by us after the response returns.
  • Your backup data (dream entries and settings) — only if you enable Google Drive backup, uploaded directly to your own Google Drive

Your dream entries themselves — the text you write — are never sent to or stored on our servers. They live on your device, and the only two ways any of that text leaves it are the AI feature and the Drive backup described above, both of which only happen when you actively use them.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Your dream entries are stored on your device. Your account identity, credit balance, and transaction/AI-usage records are stored in our backend database (Supabase), encrypted in transit, and accessible only to your signed-in account. You can delete your account and its associated records from within the app.

3. Third-Party Services

DreamLoop uses the following third-party services for specific features:

  • Google Sign-In — Used as the only sign-in method. Governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Supabase — Our backend provider for authentication and database storage. Holds your account identity, credit balance, transaction records, and the AI-usage log described above — never your dream text.
  • OpenRouter — Used to run AI features (recurring-pattern detection and the Nightmare Resolver). Only the text of the specific dream entry you're acting on is sent, only at that moment, routed to the model your request selects.
  • Google Drive — Used only if you enable Drive backup. Your backup file is uploaded directly to your own Google Drive using Google's narrowest Drive scope (drive.file), so the app can only see files it created itself — never the rest of your Drive. This backup file is not encrypted before upload (it's a plain JSON export of your data) — treat it with the same care as any file in your Drive. Governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Razorpay — Used to process credit top-up purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Razorpay; we receive only confirmation that a payment was made, not your card or bank details. See Razorpay's privacy policy.
  • Brevo — Used to send transactional emails to the address on your account (purchase confirmations, and a weekly digest if you've enabled it).

DreamLoop contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs of any kind.

4. Permissions Used

On Android, DreamLoop requests: POST_NOTIFICATIONS (for the daily dream-reminder notification), RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (so a scheduled reminder still fires after you restart your phone), and VIBRATE (notification vibration). On iOS, it requests calendar access only to power the date-picker used when logging a dream — this does not read or write your actual calendar events. DreamLoop does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

5. Credits & Payments

DreamLoop uses a credits system rather than a subscription: you buy credits in fixed INR tiers (from ₹125 for 100 credits up to ₹5,500 for 5,000 credits) via Razorpay, and a small number of AI uses are included free each month. Credits never expire and carry forward between months. We never see your card, bank, or billing details — only confirmation from Razorpay that a payment succeeded.

6. Children's Privacy

DreamLoop does not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and the app is not directed at children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of DreamLoop after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
