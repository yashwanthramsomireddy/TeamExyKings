Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

King Color Picker ("the app") is built by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy under the TeamExyKings brand. This policy explains what the app accesses on your device and what it does with it.

Camera access

The app requires camera permission to function — its entire purpose is reading the color of whatever the camera is pointed at, live. The camera feed is used only to display a live preview and sample the pixel under the on-screen crosshair; it is never recorded, saved as a photo or video, or transmitted anywhere.

What the app stores

When you tap Save, the app stores the picked color (its HEX/RGB/HSL values, a name, and the time it was saved) in the app's private storage on your device only. The list keeps your most recent 20 saves; older ones are dropped automatically. This data is never uploaded, synced to any server, or shared with any third party, unless you personally use the app's Export/Share feature to send a palette file yourself.

What King Color Picker does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no Google/Facebook login of any kind.
  • No internet connection is required for the app's core features.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs are included.
  • The camera feed is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere — only the colors you explicitly save are kept, and only on your device.

Optional links

The About screen includes optional links (website, share) that open your browser or another app. These external destinations have their own privacy practices, outside King Color Picker's control.
