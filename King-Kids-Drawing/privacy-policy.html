Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

King Kids Drawing ("the app") is built by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy under the TeamExyKings brand. This policy explains what information the app does - and does not - collect.

No account, no sign-in

The app does not require or offer any account creation or sign-in. You can use every feature without providing any personal information.

No data collection

King Kids Drawing does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics. We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or any third-party tracking tools.

Where your drawings are stored

Every drawing you create is stored only in the app's private storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to any server. If you use the Backup feature, the backup file is saved only to the location you choose on your device (or wherever you move it yourself) - King Kids Drawing never uploads it anywhere.

Device permissions

The app may ask to save images to your device's gallery when you choose to export a drawing. No other device permissions are requested, and the app does not access your contacts, location, microphone, or camera.

Children's privacy

King Kids Drawing is designed to be safe for children to use precisely because it collects no personal information, has no account system, no in-app chat, no ads, and no external links reachable from within the drawing experience.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
