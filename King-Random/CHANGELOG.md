# Changelog

All notable changes to King Random are recorded here.

## [1.0.0] — 12 November 2026

First release. versionCode 1.

### Added
- **Random number** — custom range with negative values, up to 1000 numbers per draw, "no repeats"
  mode, optional sorting, six quick range presets, swap min/max, copy and share.
- **Dice roller** — D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20, D100 presets plus custom 2–1000 sides, 1–10 dice per
  roll, pip faces for six-sided dice, tumbling roll animation, running total, share.
- **Coin flip** — 3D flip animation, 1–100 flips at a time, per-flip H/T strip, lifetime heads and
  tails counts, heads percentage, current streak and best streak, reset stats.
- **Spin wheel** — multiple named wheels, up to 30 options each, animated canvas wheel with
  per-segment labels, pointer at 12 o'clock, "remove winner after each spin" for raffle draws,
  create / rename / delete wheels.
- **List shuffler** — line- or comma-separated input, shuffle all, draw N at random, or split into
  2–10 balanced teams; named saved lists with load and delete; copy and share results.
- **History** — every result kept locally, newest first, filterable per tool, individually
  deletable, shareable as text; configurable limit of 50 / 100 / 200 / 500 entries.
- **Settings** — theme (pitch black, dark, light, system), haptic feedback, animations,
  keep screen on, history options, and JSON backup / restore through the system file picker.
- **About** — app icon with the breathing corner glow, version, build credit, and links to the
  website, privacy policy, terms of use, credits, support email and share.
- **Credits** — the tools King Random is built with.
- **FAQ** — six categories with a search box and expandable answers.
- Pitch-black AMOLED theme with a white accent as the default, matching the King app family.
- Top-right FAQ, About, History and Settings icons on the home screen.
- Adaptive launcher icon and themed splash screen.

### Notes
- No internet permission and no runtime permissions of any kind.
- No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services are present.
- All data stays in the app's private storage until you export it yourself.
