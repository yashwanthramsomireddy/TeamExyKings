King Calc — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for King Calc

Effective date: 28 September 2026
Developer: Yashwanth Ram Somireddy, TeamExyKings
Website: teamexykings.in
Contact: Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com

King Calc does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. It has no account system, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

King Calc saves the following locally, on your phone only:

  • Your calculation history
  • Your settings (theme, font, decimal places, number format)
  • Your favourite currencies
  • The most recent currency exchange rates, so the converter works offline

This data never leaves your device. You can clear your history in Settings at any time, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, files, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID.

Network requests

The only network request King Calc makes is to fetch currency exchange rates from the free ExchangeRate-API open-access service (open.er-api.com).

  • The request does not include any personal information or data you have entered.
  • As with any internet request, the rates service can see your device's IP address. We do not receive, log or use this information. Their handling is governed by their own policy.
  • If you never open the Currency screen, no request is made. If you are offline, the app uses the last saved rates.

Birthday reminders (optional)

If you switch on Birthday reminders on the Age tab, Android asks to allow notifications. King Calc then schedules two notifications per saved birthday on your phone (1 week and 1 day before, at 9:00 am). They are created and shown entirely on your device: no names or dates are sent anywhere and no internet connection is used.

Photos (optional custom background)

If you choose to set a custom calculator background, King Calc asks for access to your photos so you can pick one image. That image is copied into the app's own private storage on your phone and used only as the Calculator tab's background. It is never uploaded, shared or sent anywhere, and removing the background (or uninstalling the app) deletes it.

What is saved on your phone

Settings, calculation history, the formula you were typing, the numbers on the Loan / EMI, SIP / FD and Units tabs, the last rate on GST, Discount and VAT, and the names and dates on the Age tab are saved only on your phone so they are there next time. None of it is sent anywhere.

Backups (optional)

If you tap “Back up to a folder”, King Calc writes a JSON file (your settings, calculation history, saved inputs and Age names and dates) into a folder that you choose in Android's own folder picker. It is never uploaded or sent anywhere; it stays wherever you put it until you delete it. “Restore” only reads a file you pick.

Permissions

The only permission-gated features are the optional custom background above, which asks for photo access only when you tap “Choose image”, and optional birthday reminders, which ask to show notifications only when you switch them on. Otherwise King Calc requests no sensitive permissions: it uses internet access only for the currency rates described above, and vibration for optional key-press feedback.

Third-party services

  • ExchangeRate-API (currency rates): described above. Their terms and privacy practices apply to that request: exchangerate-api.com/terms
  • None otherwise — no advertising networks, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs are included.

Donations

The Donate screen opens a Razorpay or PayPal payment page in your browser, entirely outside the app, depending on which option you choose. King Calc does not collect, see or store any payment, card, UPI or bank information — that is handled by Razorpay or PayPal under their own privacy policies and terms. Donating is optional and does not unlock anything; every feature is already free. The on-screen thank-you message appears whenever that browser page is closed and is not a confirmation that a payment was made.

Google Play

King Calc is distributed through Google Play. Google may share anonymous, aggregated statistics with the developer, such as install counts and crash reports from users who chose to share diagnostics. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. King Calc itself does not collect it, and the app has no way to identify you.

Children's privacy

King Calc collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Open source

KingCalc is open source under the MIT License. You can inspect exactly what the app does at github.com/yashwanthramsomireddy/KingCalc.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted in the repository and on teamexykings.in with a new effective date.
