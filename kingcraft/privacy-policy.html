King Craft: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. App by TeamExyKings (Yashwanth Ram Somireddy).

Summary

King Craft works entirely on your phone. It has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, receive, store or share any of your data.

Your files

PDFs, photos and scans you choose are processed on your device only. Temporary working copies are kept in the app's private cache and are deleted when the app starts again. Files you save go to Downloads/KingCraft or Pictures/KingCraft on your device, where you control them.

What the app stores on your device

Only your settings (theme, accent colour, font, default quality), your own resize presets, and a short list of recently saved files (names and locations). Nothing leaves the device. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Permissions

  • No INTERNET permission. The app cannot send data over the network.
  • Camera: optional. Lets you scan a page. Pictures are used only inside the app and are not uploaded.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only): asked only when you tap Save.
  • The page scanner is Google's on-device document scanner provided by Google Play services; Google's own terms apply to it.

Photos and files are chosen with Android's own pickers, which need no permission, and the app only sees what you pick. You can allow or deny the camera at any time in Android settings.

Text recognition (OCR)

OCR uses an on-device model bundled in the app. Images are not uploaded.

Donations and links

The donate, website and email links open your browser or email app. Any payment is handled by Razorpay or PayPal under their own policies. King Craft does not see payment details.

Children

King Craft collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
