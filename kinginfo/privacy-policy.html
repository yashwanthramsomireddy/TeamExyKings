Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

King Info ("the app") is built by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy under the TeamExyKings brand. This policy explains what the app reads from your device and what it does with it.

What King Info reads from your device

The app reads standard device, hardware and system information that Android exposes through its public APIs: device model and manufacturer, CPU details, display and GPU information, battery status, Android version and build details, camera characteristics, thermal sensor readings, the list of hardware sensors and their live values, supported media codecs, and standard storage directory paths. None of this requires an account or a network connection.

Optional permissions

  • Location (coarse) — used only to show your Wi-Fi network's real name (SSID) in the Network section, which Android hides without it. King Info does not use this to track or record your location.
  • Phone state — used only to show network connection type (e.g. LTE, 5G) in the Network section.
  • Bluetooth (Android 12+) — used only to show the Bluetooth adapter's name, where applicable.

All three are optional. Denying any of them simply means the one related field shows an explanation instead of a value — nothing else in the app is affected, and these permissions are never used for tracking, advertising, or analytics.

What King Info does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no Google/Facebook login of any kind.
  • No internet connection is required for the app's core features.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs are included.
  • Nothing read by the app is stored, uploaded, synced, or sent anywhere — every reading is shown live and discarded when you leave the screen, except what you explicitly copy or share yourself.
  • King Info does not, and cannot, read your device's IMEI, serial number, or other apps' data.

Optional links

The About screen includes optional links (website, share) that open your browser or another app. These external destinations have their own privacy practices, outside King Info's control.
