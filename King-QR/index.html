What it does

Scan anything

QR codes plus Aztec, Data Matrix, PDF417, EAN, UPC, Code 39/93/128, ITF and Codabar. Point the camera and it reads automatically.

Understands what it reads

Links, Wi-Fi networks, UPI payment requests, contact cards, email, phone and SMS are recognised and laid out field by field.

Create your own

Build QR codes for text, links, Wi-Fi details, UPI requests and contacts, then save them to your gallery or share them straight away.

Searchable history

Everything you scan and generate is kept on the phone, with search, filters and starred entries. Clear it whenever you like.

Pitch black by default

A true-black AMOLED theme that saves power, plus Dark, Light and System modes and seven accent colours.

Backup and restore

Export your history as a JSON file and bring it back on a new phone — no account and no cloud involved.

Privacy, in plain terms

  • No internet permission. King QR cannot send anything anywhere, because it never asks for network access.
  • The camera is only a reader. Frames are checked for codes and discarded. No photo or video is ever recorded or saved.
  • Your history stays on your phone, in the app's own private storage. There is no account, no sync and no analytics.
  • Nothing is collected. No identifiers, no usage statistics, no advertising SDKs.
  • UPI codes are just codes. King QR builds and reads them. It never touches, holds or moves money.

Requirements

  • Android 10 (API 29) or newer
  • Camera permission — the only permission the app requests
  • Around 15 MB of storage
