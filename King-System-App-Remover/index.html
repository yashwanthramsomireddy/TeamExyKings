King System App Remover

A clean, ad-free way to see and remove the apps on your phone.
Free forever No ads No sign-in No tracking Pitch-black AMOLED

What it can and cannot do. Android only lets an app remove apps you installed yourself. System and pre-installed apps are listed here for reference, with a shortcut to their app info screen where you can disable them — but no app can uninstall them without root, and King System App Remover never asks for root.

What it does

Batch uninstall

Tick any number of your own apps and work through them in one pass, with a queue you can stop at any time.

Real sizes

App, data and cache together — not just the APK — when optional usage access is on.

Last used

See which apps you haven't opened in a month, so the dead weight is easy to spot.

Sort and filter

By name, size, install date, last updated, last used or package. Filters for unused, large, recent and disabled apps.

Storage freed

A running total of the space you've reclaimed, kept on your device.

Share the list

Export your installed-app list as a plain text file whenever you need a record.

Permissions, in plain words

  • All installed apps — listing and removing your apps is the whole purpose of the app, so Android requires this to show them.
  • Request delete packages — lets the app hand a package to Android's own uninstall dialog. You still confirm every removal yourself.
  • Usage access — optional, granted by you in your phone's settings, used only for last-used dates and real app sizes.

Nothing is uploaded. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.
