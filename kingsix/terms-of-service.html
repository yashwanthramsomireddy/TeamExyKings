Free and Pro

Free accounts can view matches and scorecards, open the sample match and open matches shared with them. King Six Pro (a yearly subscription) lets you score matches, create teams, players and series, back up to the cloud and use all analysis. Features and prices can change over time; the price shown in Google Play at purchase applies.

Subscriptions and billing

Pro is billed yearly through Google Play, renews automatically until you cancel, and can be managed or cancelled any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. Refunds follow Google Play's policy. If your yearly subscription ends, Pro keeps working for a further 7 days (grace week) so you can renew. After that you can still view everything you scored, but scoring and creating new teams, players, series and matches are locked until you renew. Your data is not deleted when Pro ends.

Your backups

King Six keeps your data in your account and can make backup files, but you are responsible for keeping your own copy of anything important. We recommend saving a backup file to your Google Drive about once a month; the app reminds you unless you switch the reminders off. We are not liable for data lost because no copy was kept, to the extent the law allows.

Reminders

The app may show reminders about renewals and backups. You can switch them off in Settings.
