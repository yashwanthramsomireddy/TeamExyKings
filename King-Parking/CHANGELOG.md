# Changelog

All notable changes to King Parking are recorded here.

## [1.0.0] — 12 November 2026

**versionCode 1 · first release**

### Added
- Save a parking spot with its GPS position and the accuracy of the fix.
- Optional bay photo taken in the app, stored privately and auto-shrunk and straightened.
- Place name, "Level / bay" and free-text note fields on every spot.
- Multiple active spots at once, each showing how long it has been parked.
- Distance from your current position on the home screen and the spot screen.
- "Walk back to my vehicle" — walking directions handed to Google Maps, with a plain maps pin and a web fallback.
- "Open in Maps" and copy-coordinates on the spot screen.
- Share a spot as text plus a maps link, with the bay photo attached when there is one.
- Edit the place, bay and note of a saved spot.
- "I found my vehicle" clears a spot; history keeps it so you can park there again.
- History list with restore and delete, and a one-tap Clear.
- Settings: theme (pitch black, dark grey, light, follow system), 7 accent colours, metric or imperial distances, precise-fix wait, photo prompt, keep-history toggle.
- Backup to a single JSON file and restore with merge or replace.
- Delete all data in one action.
- FAQ with categories and search; About screen with website, privacy policy, terms, credits, support and share.
- Pitch-black AMOLED theme with a white accent by default.

### Notes
- No internet permission, no analytics, no tracking, no account.
- minSdk 29 (Android 10), targetSdk 36.
