Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version. King GPS Camera collects nothing, sends nothing and stores nothing about you anywhere other than on your own phone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your photos and your location stay on your device unless you share them yourself.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the Android application King GPS Camera (package com.teamexykings.kinggpscamera), published by TeamExyKings. It does not cover any other app or website.

2. Information the app collects

None. The developer receives no personal information from this app. There is no server, no database and no account system behind it. Nothing you do in the app is reported anywhere.

Specifically, the app does not collect or transmit your name, email address, phone number, device identifiers, advertising ID, contacts, installed-app list, usage statistics or crash reports.

3. Information the app uses on your device

Some information is read and used locally so that the app can do its job. It is processed on the phone and is not sent to the developer.

Camera The camera feed is used to show the viewfinder and to take the photo you ask for. Images are written only to the location described in section 5.
Location Your coordinates, altitude, accuracy and bearing are read so they can be drawn onto the photo and, if you leave the option on, written into the photo's EXIF metadata. The app keeps no location history and no log of where you have been.
Settings Your stamp preferences are stored locally on the device and are removed when the app is uninstalled.

4. The one network request the app makes

If you have the place name or the full address switched on, the app asks Android's built-in geocoding service to turn your coordinates into a readable address. On most devices that service is provided by Google and the lookup involves a network request made by Android itself, not by this app.

That request contains the coordinates being looked up. It is handled under the privacy policy of whoever supplies geocoding on your device — typically Google's privacy policy. The developer neither sees nor receives the result.

You can switch this off. Turn off both Place headline and Full address in Settings and the app makes no network request at all. Everything else — coordinates, altitude, accuracy, date and time — works entirely offline.

5. Where your photos go

Finished photos are written to your device's own picture storage, in a folder named King GPS Camera inside Pictures. They are ordinary JPEG files that your gallery and file manager can see.

They are never uploaded. If you use the share button, the photo goes wherever you send it, and from that point the receiving app's own privacy policy applies.

If Write GPS into EXIF is on, your coordinates and the time of capture are stored inside the image file as standard EXIF tags, the same as your phone's own camera app does. Please keep in mind that anyone you send the photo to can read that information, and that the stamp printed on the picture is visible to anyone who sees it. Turn the EXIF option off, or switch individual stamp fields off, if you would rather not share your location.

6. Permissions and why they exist

CAMERA Required. Shows the viewfinder and takes the photo.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 Optional. Supplies the coordinates stamped onto the photo. Decline it and the app still works as a date-and-time stamp camera.
INTERNET
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		 Used only for the address lookup described in section 4.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Requested only on Android 8 and 9, to save the photo into your gallery. Not requested on Android 10 and above.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your phone's Settings under Apps → King GPS Camera → Permissions.

7. Third parties

The app contains no advertising network, no analytics library and no social media SDK. Nothing in it profiles you or builds an audience segment.

It does use Google Play services location on devices that have it, to get a better position fix. That component is part of the Google Play services framework already on your phone and operates under Google's own privacy policy. On devices without Google Play services the app falls back to Android's built-in location provider.

8. Children

King GPS Camera is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children either.

9. Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, deletion and portability apply to data a developer holds about you. Since no data about you is held anywhere outside your own phone, there is nothing to request, export or delete.

Everything the app has stored is removed from your device when you uninstall it. Photos you have already saved stay in your gallery, as you would expect — delete them from there if you want them gone.

10. Security

Because nothing is transmitted or stored off-device, there is no server to breach. Your photos and settings are protected by your phone's own security — your screen lock and Android's app sandbox.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date. If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this policy will say so plainly and before the fact.
