Delete your King Log data

by TeamExyKings · Last updated 31 October 2026 · applies to King Log 1.8 and newer

In short: King Log has no accounts and no servers. We never receive your data, so there is no King Log account to close and nothing of yours on our side for us to delete. Your data can be in three places: on your phone, in files you saved (backups and exports), and, only if you connected it, in the KingLog folder in your own Google Drive. Each one is yours to remove, and the steps below take about a minute. Once deleted, the data cannot be brought back, by you or by us.

1. On your phone

  • Delete everything (data and settings): in the app, Settings ▸ Reset everything. Type RESET to confirm. This deletes every Vault entry, Finance month, EMI plan item, vehicle and fill-up, puts all settings back to default, and removes the app lock, the backup key and the Google Drive link from the phone. King Log then opens as if it were newly installed.
  • Delete the data but keep your settings: Settings ▸ Your data ▸ Erase all data on this phone.
  • Or in Android: Settings ▸ Apps ▸ King Log ▸ Storage ▸ Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

2. In your Google Drive (only if you connected it)

  • In the app: Settings ▸ Backup & restore ▸ Delete Drive backups. This deletes the KingLog folder that King Log created in your Drive and everything in it. Nothing else in your Drive is touched, because King Log cannot even see your other files.
  • Or in Drive: open drive.google.com, move the KingLog folder to the bin, then empty the bin.
  • Remove King Log's access: myaccount.google.com/permissions ▸ King Log ▸ Remove access.

3. In files you saved

These are ordinary files, not stored inside King Log, so uninstalling the app or using Reset everything does not remove them. This is deliberate: a backup should never be lost by accident. Delete them with your Files app:

  • Backups on the phone: the Documents/KingLog folder. Some phones remove it together with the app's data, others keep it, so check.
  • A folder you chose for a safe copy (for example on an SD card or in a cloud-synced folder): delete the KingLog folder inside it.
  • Exports and your recovery key file: Fuel or EMI exports and the key file you saved are ordinary files in the place you chose, usually Downloads.

4. What happens to your recovery key

The backup key lives only on your phone and in the copy you saved. Reset everything removes it from the phone. Encrypted backups that you keep can afterwards be opened only with the key file you saved. If you delete both the backups and the key file, nothing is left that anyone could read.

5. Want it in writing?

We run no server and receive no data from the app, so there is nothing for us to delete. We never ask for your Vault, bank or card details; please do not send them.
