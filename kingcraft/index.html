King Craft

PDF and photo tools that work fully offline.

Merge, edit, sign and fill PDFs, resize, convert and edit photos, read text from images and turn scans into PDFs. Free forever, no ads, no account, and the app has no internet permission, so your files never leave your phone.

What it does

PDF Tools

Merge, split or extract pages, reorder, rotate and delete, compress, watermark, page numbers, lock and unlock, extract text, and turn photos or camera scans into a PDF (Image to PDF).

Edit, sign and fill forms

Add signatures, text and pictures to any page, resize them, and save once. Fill the boxes in PDF forms (text, tick boxes, drop-downs) and optionally flatten them.

Signatures

Draw and keep up to 10 signatures on your phone, ready to place on any PDF. They never leave the app.

Image Tools

Resize to exact pixels, cm, mm or inches or to a maximum file size in KB; convert between JPEG, PNG and WebP in batches; edit photos with rotate, flip, crop, brightness, contrast, saturation and black and white.

OCR

Turn a photo or scan into editable text on your phone. English.

Private by design

  • No internet permission: the app cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no account.
  • Pitch Black, Dark, White, System and Custom themes with an accent colour picker.

Support

King Craft is free. If it saved you time you can leave a voluntary donation from the Settings or About screen in the app. Questions or bugs: email us, or use Support / Contact in the app's About screen.

Signing places a drawn image like signing on paper; it is not a certified digital signature. Changing a digitally signed PDF (for example unlocking a government e-document) makes its signature show as invalid, so keep the original. Resize presets are examples, not official requirements.
