Refund Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026
In short. Credits are one-time purchases through Google Play. There is no subscription and nothing renews automatically.

If something went wrong

Charged but no credits? Credits wrong? E-mail Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com with your Google Play order number. We fix it or refund you.

Used credits

Credits already spent on a finished AI result or export can’t be refunded. If an AI request fails you aren’t charged.

Google Play refunds

Payments are processed by Google Play, and Google’s refund rules also apply (for example, refund requests within its published window). You can request one from your Play order history. Refunded purchases have their credits removed.

Deleting your account

Remaining credits are forfeited when an account is deleted.
