Privacy Policy for Bookmark Tab Manager

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Collection and Usage

Bookmark Tab Manager value your privacy. All your bookmarks, settings, and workspace preferences are stored locally in your browser's storage on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data or browsing history to any external servers.

2. Google Drive Backup Integration

Our application offers an optional feature allowing users to back up their data directly to their own Google Drive account. This integration uses the narrowest possible permission scope (drive.file). Bookmark Tab Manager can only access, create, or modify the specific backup files created by this app. We do not have access to any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to third parties. Your data never leaves your local browser session unless you explicitly initiate a manual backup to your Google Drive.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our open-source repository or community support channels listed on teamexykings.in.
