King Estate – Privacy Policy

App: King Estate (com.teamexykings.kingestate) · Developer: Yashwanth Ram Somireddy (TeamExyKings) · Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Version 1.3.0

In short: King Estate works entirely on your phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics.

1. About this app

King Estate is a free property-document checklist for Indian property transactions. It also includes a Building Kata calculator that estimates construction materials and compares dealer quotes you type in.

2. Information we collect

None. The developer never receives any information from the app. Everything you enter stays on your device:

  • checklists, document statuses and personal notes;
  • reminder dates you set;
  • your chosen state, theme and accent colour;
  • values typed into the Building Kata calculator (area, dealer names and rates), which are not even saved after you close the app.

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone and is not uploaded, synced or backed up to any cloud by us.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+): used only to show reminders you create yourself. You can decline; the app still works.
  • King Estate does not request internet, contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage or phone permissions.

4. Files you create

When you choose to export a PDF checklist or save a JSON backup, the file is created on your device. You decide whether to share or store it, and with whom (for example by email or WhatsApp). Once you share a file, the receiving app's privacy policy applies. Keep backups safe: they contain your notes.

5. Links to other websites

Some screens show addresses of government portals (for example registration or RERA websites) and links to our website. Opening one launches your browser. Those sites are not controlled by us and have their own policies. The app itself makes no network connection.

6. Analytics, ads and tracking

None. There are no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, session-recording or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Fonts are your phone's built-in system fonts.

7. Children

King Estate is intended for adults dealing with property matters and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

8. Your control and data deletion

  • Delete everything: Settings → Clear All Data, or uninstall the app.
  • Settings → Request data deletion also lets you email us. If you have ever emailed us, we will delete that correspondence on request within 30 days.
  • Settings → Email unsubscribe lets you ask to be removed from any TeamExyKings emails (we send none from the app).

Because the developer holds no data from the app, there is nothing to access, correct or export from our side. Use Settings → Create backup to take your own copy.

9. Security

Data stays in Android's app-private storage and is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. We still recommend using a screen lock on your phone and avoiding saving sensitive numbers in notes.

10. Not legal or construction advice

Checklists and calculator outputs are general guides only. Requirements vary by state and property, and calculator figures are indicative thumb rules. Consult a qualified lawyer, registrar or structural engineer before acting. King Estate is not affiliated with any government body.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example if a feature ever uses the internet), we will update this page and the date above, and describe the change in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
