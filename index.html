About TeamExyKings

TeamExyKings is a developer collective. We started out building custom ROMs and kernels for Samsung Galaxy devices — spanning the Note, S, A and Tab series from roughly the Galaxy S5 through the S10 and Note 10 era — before shifting focus to free, open-source tools and browser extensions.

Our current tools are actively maintained by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy. Our ROM and kernel work was built together by the wider TeamExyKings developer team — see the full list above.
