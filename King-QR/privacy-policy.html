Privacy Policy

King QR · TeamExyKings
Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version: King QR collects nothing. It has no internet permission, no account and no analytics. Everything you scan or create stays on your phone until you delete it.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to the King QR Android application (com.teamexykings.kingqr), published by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy under the TeamExyKings brand.

2. Information we collect

None. King QR does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Specifically, the app does not gather:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or any account details — there is no sign-in
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or location data
  • Usage statistics, analytics, crash telemetry or diagnostics
  • The contents of the codes you scan or create

The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending data off your device.

3. Camera

King QR requests the CAMERA permission for one purpose: to look for QR codes and barcodes in the live camera feed while you are on the Scan screen.

  • Detection runs entirely on your device using an on-device model. No frame is uploaded anywhere.
  • No photo or video is recorded, and nothing from the camera is written to storage.
  • The camera is released as soon as you leave the Scan screen or close the app.
  • You can refuse or revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings. Code generation continues to work without it.

4. Data stored on your device

The following is saved in the app's own private storage, readable only by King QR:

  • History — the text of codes you scan and generate, with the type, format and timestamp
  • Settings — your theme, accent colour and scanning preferences

You can turn history off in Settings, delete individual entries, or clear everything from the History screen or Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

5. Images you save or share

When you save a generated QR code, the image is written to Pictures/King QR on your device, where your gallery app can see it. When you use Share, the image is passed to the app you choose. From that point on, the receiving app's own privacy policy governs what happens to it.

6. Backup files

If you export your history, the file is written to the location you pick using the Android file picker. That file is yours to keep, move or delete. King QR does not upload it, and has no copy of it.

7. Android system backup

If you have Android's own backup enabled, the system may include King QR's settings and history in your Google account backup. That backup is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by King QR. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

8. UPI codes

King QR can read and build UPI payment QR codes. It only encodes and decodes the text of such a code. It never initiates, processes, holds or transfers any payment, has no connection to any bank or payment provider, and cannot see whether a payment happened.

9. Links you open

When you tap Open on a scanned link, King QR hands it to your browser or another app of your choice. What happens next is governed by that app and that website, not by King QR. Always check a link before following it — a QR code can encode any address.

10. Third-party components

King QR uses Google ML Kit's on-device barcode scanning and the ZXing encoding library. Both run locally on your device within the app, with no network calls from King QR. The app contains no advertising, attribution or analytics SDKs.

11. Children

King QR is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, children included.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's changelog.
