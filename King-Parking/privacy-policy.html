Privacy Policy

King Parking (com.teamexykings.kingparking) · TeamExyKings
Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version: King Parking collects nothing. The app is installed without the internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere even if it wanted to. Everything you save stays in the app's private storage on your phone.

1. Who we are

King Parking is developed and published by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy under the TeamExyKings brand. You can reach us at Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting service in the app.

3. Information the app stores on your device

So that the app can do its job, the following is written to the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Parking positions — the latitude, longitude and accuracy of each spot you choose to save, with the date and time.
  • Your text — the place name, level or bay and note you type for a spot.
  • Bay photos — photos you choose to take inside the app. They are stored privately and do not appear in your gallery or in other apps.
  • Settings — your theme, accent colour, unit and saving preferences.

This data never leaves your device unless you deliberately share a spot or write a backup file yourself. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

4. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) — read only while you are saving a parking spot or refreshing the distance to one, so the app can record and compare the position. The app does not request background location access and does not track your movement.
  • Camera (CAMERA) — used only when you tap the button to take a bay photo. The app does not open the camera at any other time.

King Parking does not request the INTERNET permission.

5. Children's privacy

King Parking is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

6. Third parties

There are no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking libraries in the app. When you choose to open a spot in a maps application or share a spot with another app, that action hands the position to an app you selected, and that app's own privacy policy applies to what it does next. King Parking has no control over and no visibility into those apps.

If you install the app from Google Play, Google collects installation and device data in the ordinary course of running the store. That is covered by Google's own privacy policy, not by this one.

7. Backups

Settings offers a backup that writes a single JSON file to a location you pick. That file is yours; it is not uploaded anywhere by the app. If your phone's system backup is switched on, Android may include the app's data in your own device backup — that process belongs to Android and your account, not to us.

8. Your control over your data

  • Delete a single spot from the spot screen.
  • Clear history from the home screen.
  • Remove everything at once with Settings → Delete all data.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all of it, including photos.

9. Security

Your saved spots and photos live in the app's private storage, which Android keeps isolated from other applications. No copy exists outside your device.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
