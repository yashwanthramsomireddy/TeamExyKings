King Random

Numbers, dice, coin, wheel and shuffle — in one small app.

Free forever No ads No tracking Works offline No sign-in

Five tools

Random number

Any range you type, including negatives. Draw up to 1000 numbers at once, with or without repeats, sorted or in draw order.

Dice roller

D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20 and D100 one tap away, or type any custom number of sides. Roll up to ten dice and see the total.

Coin flip

A proper flip animation, single or multi-flip, with lifetime heads/tails counts, current streak and best streak.

Spin wheel

Build wheels of your own options and let one decide. Keep several wheels, and optionally remove the winner after each spin for raffle-style draws.

List shuffler

Paste a list and shuffle it, draw a few names at random, or split everyone into balanced teams. Save lists you reuse.

History & backup

Every result is kept locally so you can check what came up. Back everything up to a single JSON file whenever you want.

Built the way the rest of the King apps are

  • Pitch-black AMOLED theme with a white accent, plus dark, light and system options
  • No internet permission at all — nothing can leave your device
  • No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash-reporting services
  • Native Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, built for recent Android versions
  • Haptics and animations you can switch off
