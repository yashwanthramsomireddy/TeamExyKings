King Six — Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 (app version 1.8.0) · App by TeamExyKings (Yashwanth Ram Somireddy)

The short version

King Six needs a Google sign-in. Your teams, players, matches and scores are stored only on your phone, in the app's own database (SQLite), and are not uploaded to our servers. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

Your Google account's email address and a unique account id (from Google Sign-In, to know who you are), and, if you subscribe, a record of your Pro subscription (the purchase token Google Play gives us and its dates). The team names, player names, nicknames and scores you enter stay on your phone and are not sent to us.

What the owner can see

To run the service and support you, the owner of King Six can see, in a private admin screen protected by a server-side check, a list of accounts with email address, display name, join and last sign-in dates, plan (Free, Pro, grace or expired) and subscription dates, plus a record of each paid period. The owner can send a renewal reminder to accounts that have not renewed. The owner cannot see your teams, players or matches, because they never leave your phone.

Notifications

King Six can show reminders on your phone: Pro renewal reminders (daily in the last 7 days of your paid year and during the 7-day grace period after it), a monthly reminder for Pro users to save a backup copy, and a renewal reminder sent by the owner if you have not renewed. They are created on your phone from your plan dates; no push-messaging service is used and nothing is sent to your email. Android asks for permission first and you can switch each kind off in Settings > Reminders.

Backups and Excel export (Pro)

With King Six Pro you can create a backup file of your teams, players and matches, and export your whole profile (your numbers, your matches, every match, every team and every player) to an Excel file. Once a month the app can also save a backup copy on your phone (inside the app, or in a folder you choose). You choose where a file is saved with Android's file picker, for example Google Drive. King Six does not connect to your Google Drive, cannot see it and only writes the file you ask for. You can restore a backup file you already have on any plan.

Where it is stored

Your cricket data is stored only on this phone. Sign-in and subscription records are kept in our authentication and database service provided by Supabase; each account can read only its own record.

Sign-in and subscriptions

Sign-in is handled by Google. Payment is handled by Google Play: we never see your card. When you subscribe, the app sends the purchase token to our server, which checks it with Google Play to confirm your plan. A hashed form of your account id is attached to the purchase so it cannot be used on someone else's account.

What the app does not do

It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. It reads or writes a file only when you choose one in Android's file picker (a backup, a restore or an Excel export). It contains no advertising or tracking software.

Sharing

When you share a scorecard, King Six hands a text, image, PDF or CSV file to the Android share sheet and you choose where it goes.

Fonts

The Nunito font is bundled inside the app; no font is downloaded from anyone.

No tracking

King Six has no analytics, advertising, session recording, keystroke logging or crash-reporting service. The only network requests are sign-in and subscription checks described above.

Children

King Six is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. You confirm you are 13 or older when you sign in; where the law sets a higher age, a parent or guardian must agree. Add a player only with their permission, or a parent's or guardian's permission for a child. If you believe a child's data was added by mistake, email us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete my data permanently deletes your account on our server and erases all teams, players and matches from this phone. It does not touch backup files you saved to Google Drive or elsewhere. Signing out keeps your data on the phone. Uninstalling the app erases the data on the phone, so Pro users should keep a backup file. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription: cancel that in Google Play first.
