Refund Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: everything is bought through Google Play, and Google Play handles refunds. Plans and top-ups are one-time purchases. Nothing renews automatically.

1. What you buy

A plan or a top-up is a one-time purchase of credits. Plan credits roll over while the plan stays active; top-up credits do not expire. There is no subscription that renews by itself, so there is nothing to cancel.

2. How to ask for a refund

  • Use Google Play: open your order history on the Play Store (Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history), choose the order and select Request a refund. Google decides and pays the refund under its refund policy.
  • Or write to us at yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com with your registered email and the Google Play order number. We look at every request, but refunds are paid by Google Play.

3. What happens to your credits

  • When Google refunds a purchase, the credits that came with it are removed from your account.
  • If some of those credits were already used, your balance can go below zero until it is covered by the next purchase. Free models keep working.
  • Credits that were already spent on scans are not returned, because the AI work has already been done.

4. Statutory rights

Nothing here limits any right you have under the consumer law where you live.
