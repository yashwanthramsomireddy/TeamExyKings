What it does

Back up in bulk

Tick as many apps as you like and save them in one run, with live progress and a summary at the end.

Your folder, your rules

You pick one folder. The app writes there and nowhere else — it never asks for access to the rest of your storage.

Split apps handled

Apps delivered as App Bundles are archived complete as a single .apks file, so a restore puts everything back.

Restore and share

Install any archived package again, pass it to another device, or clear old copies out — all from the Archived tab.

Built for AMOLED

Pitch black by default, with Dark and Light themes if you prefer them.

Nothing leaves the phone

No internet permission, no account, no analytics. Everything happens on your device.

How to use it

  1. Pick a backup folder. On first launch, choose where APK files should be written.
  2. Select your apps. Search or sort the Installed list, then tick what you want.
  3. Tap BACKUP. Each selected app is copied to your folder.
  4. Restore when you need to. On the Archived tab, pick a backup and tap INSTALL.

One important limit

Without root access, Android only allows an app's installer package — the APK — to be copied. App data, logins, settings and game saves are not part of a backup, so a restored app starts out signed out and empty. That is an Android restriction, not a missing feature.
