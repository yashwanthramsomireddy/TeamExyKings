King Travel

Pack once, tick it off, reset it for the next trip.

Free forever No ads No tracking Works offline No account

What it does

Trip templates

Start from Beach holiday, Business trip, Weekend getaway, International, Family, Trek or Pilgrimage — or from a blank list.

Tick as you pack

Items are grouped by category with a live progress bar, so you always know what is still left in the pile.

Reset and reuse

One tap unticks the whole list and keeps the items, ready for the next trip without building it again.

Your own items

Add anything with a quantity and category, edit or remove it later, or pull extra items in from another template.

Many trips at once

Keep a separate checklist per trip, archive the ones that are done, and duplicate a list you want to reuse as-is.

Backup in JSON

Export every trip to a plain JSON file and restore it on another phone — merge it in or replace what is there.

Privacy in one line

  • Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device.
  • No sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics and no advertising SDKs.
  • No permissions are requested, and the app does not use the internet.
  • Backup files go only where you choose to save them.
