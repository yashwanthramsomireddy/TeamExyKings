Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

QuickOff strips away the bloat and gives you exactly what matters — fast, reliable power actions at your fingertips.

⚡

One-Click Actions

Shutdown, restart, sleep, hibernate, lock, and log off — all accessible with a single click from the system tray icon.

⏱

Scheduled Shutdown

Set a timer to automatically shut down or restart your PC after a set number of minutes. Perfect for downloads and renders.

🪟

System Tray Native

Lives quietly in your notification area. No persistent window, no clutter — just right-click when you need it.

🌑

Pitch-Black UI

AMOLED-friendly dark theme throughout. Easy on the eyes at any hour, consistent with the TeamExyKings aesthetic.

🔒

No Data Collection

QuickOff is fully offline. It never connects to the internet, never collects telemetry, and never phones home.

🛠

Setup Utility

A built-in setup wizard helps you configure autostart on login, hotkey preferences, and default actions in seconds.
