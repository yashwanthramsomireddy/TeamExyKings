Privacy Policy

King Browser · Last updated: 9 November 2026

King Browser is developed by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy under the TeamExyKings brand. This policy explains what King Browser does and does not do with your data.

What King Browser does not do

  • No account or sign-in is required to use the app.
  • No analytics or usage tracking is built into the app.
  • No advertising, and no data is sold or shared with advertisers.
  • No data about the pages you visit is sent to TeamExyKings or any third party by the app itself.

Internet permission

King Browser requests the Internet permission because it is a web browser — loading any page you navigate to requires network access. This permission is used only to load the pages you choose to visit, fetch your chosen search engine's results, and (for the BTM extension) its own normal operation. No other permission is requested by the app.

What's stored on your device

Your bookmarks, browsing history, downloaded-file list, and app settings (theme, font, accent colour, default search engine) are stored locally on your device only. None of this is uploaded anywhere by King Browser automatically. A local Backup you create is saved only to the location you choose on your own device or cloud storage.

Optional Google Drive backup

King Browser works fully without ever signing in. If you choose to, Settings has an optional "Sign in with Google" / "Backup to Drive" feature. If you use it:

  • You sign in with your Google account using Google's own sign-in flow. King Browser never sees or stores your Google password.
  • King Browser requests access only to a private, app-specific storage area of your Google Drive (the "appDataFolder" scope) — not your regular Drive files, and not visible to you or any other app in your normal Drive file list.
  • Tapping "Backup to Drive" uploads the same bookmarks/settings data as the local Backup feature to that private area. Tapping "Restore from Drive" downloads it back.
  • This only happens when you tap those buttons — nothing is backed up automatically or in the background.
  • You can sign out at any time from Settings, and can revoke King Browser's Drive access at any time from your Google Account's security settings.

Private tabs

Pages visited in a private tab are not added to your browsing history.

Extensions

King Browser bundles the Bookmark Tab Manager (BTM) extension, enabled by default. BTM operates within the pages you browse to provide its bookmark/tab-management features; it does not add any additional data collection beyond what's described in this policy.

Third-party websites

Like any browser, King Browser lets you visit third-party websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
