Privacy Policy

Last updated:

King Sudoku, developed by TeamExyKings, is built to work entirely offline and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

King Sudoku does not require an account or sign-in. It does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or any third-party tracking. We do not collect your name, email, device identifiers, or location.

Data Storage

All game progress, settings, and statistics are stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server, because King Sudoku does not use a server.

Backup Files

The app lets you export a backup JSON file containing your settings, statistics, and current game. This file is saved only where you choose on your own device (or a location you pick via the system file picker) and is never sent anywhere automatically.

Permissions

King Sudoku requests no special device permissions beyond what the Android system requires to let you pick a file location for Backup/Restore.

Children's Privacy

King Sudoku does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data from anyone at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
