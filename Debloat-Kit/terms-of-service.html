2.What DebloatKit does

DebloatKit is a Windows utility that uses Android Debug Bridge (ADB) to list the apps installed on a connected Samsung Galaxy device and, at your request, to disable, uninstall for the current user (--user 0) or re-enable / restore selected packages. It does not require root, does not modify the system partition, and keeps the original app files on the device so that apps can be restored. The built-in safety ratings and descriptions are informational guidance only.

  • DebloatKit is built for Samsung Galaxy devices. Use on other devices is unsupported.

4.Use at your own risk

Disabling or removing system apps can cause apps or features to stop working, change how the phone behaves, affect updates, or in rare cases cause instability such as boot or lock-screen problems. Specifically:

  • Apps rated Caution, Locked or Core carry more risk. Core apps are in a separate tab and ask for confirmation for a reason. Do not disable something you do not understand.
  • Ratings are based on community experience and may not be right for your model, region, carrier build or firmware version, and apps can change between updates.
  • Before every action DebloatKit saves an automatic backup, and you can use Re-enable Selected or Panic Restore. These work while the phone is connected and ADB is working. They cannot help if the phone can no longer boot or connect. We cannot promise that every app will restore perfectly, and app data cleared by an uninstall may not return.
  • Your device manufacturer, carrier or employer may treat changes like these differently with respect to warranty, support or device-management policies. Check your own terms. We make no statement about your warranty or Knox status.
  • You should keep your own backups of anything important (photos, messages, app data) before making changes.

You are solely responsible for the apps you choose to disable, uninstall or restore.
