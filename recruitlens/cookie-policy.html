Cookie Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: we do not use cookies, advertising or tracking on this website, and the Recruit Lens app does not use cookies or any analytics or session-recording tool.

1. This website

These pages are plain documents. They do not set cookies, do not load fonts, scripts or images from other companies, and do not run analytics. Because nothing here needs your consent, there is no cookie banner.

2. The app

The Android app does not use browser cookies. It stores your sign-in on your phone (in the phone's protected storage) so you stay signed in, and it remembers your theme and colour choice there. Nothing is recorded about what you tap or type, and no screen recording is made.

3. If this changes

If we ever add analytics or any cookie that is not strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent first and update this page before we do.
