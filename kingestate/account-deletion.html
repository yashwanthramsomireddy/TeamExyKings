King Estate – Data deletion

App: King Estate (com.teamexykings.kingestate) · Developer: Yashwanth Ram Somireddy (TeamExyKings) · Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Version 1.4.0

In short: King Estate keeps your data only on your phone. You can delete all of it yourself, and we hold nothing on our servers.

Delete data on your phone

  1. Open King Estate > Settings > Data privacy > Clear All Data and confirm. This removes all checklists, notes, reminders and progress.
  2. Files you attached are in your Downloads folder under King Estate. Delete that folder with a file manager if you no longer want them.
  3. Uninstalling the app also removes its data.

Ask us to delete anything we hold

The app sends us nothing, so we hold no app data. If you have emailed us, write to Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com with the subject "King Estate data deletion request" and we will delete that correspondence within 30 days.
